---
title: "Use Ari for formula work"
description: "Ask Ari to explain saved formulas, validate a proposed rule, update a visible Model calculation, and verify the result."
---

Ari can help you understand, review, and change formulas in cfo.ai. Describe the business rule in plain language instead of writing the full expression yourself.

## What Ari can do

Ari can:

- Find and explain formulas on a Variable
- Check how a formula changes across segments or formula ranges.
- Answer questions from existing saved Model data.
- Validate a supported proposed change without applying it.
- Validate formula syntax and references
- Create or update formulas
- Apply a formula to a table, Dimension, segment, Actuals, or Forecast.
- Read calculated values after a change

## Decide whether to save

Tell Ari whether you are exploring or changing the model.

For analysis based on saved information:

> Explain the existing contribution margin by Department without changing the Model.

For a persistent change:

> Add Gross Profit to the operating plan and calculate it as Revenue minus Cost of Goods Sold in Main.

If you do not want a write, say so directly. Ari can inspect existing values or validate a supported draft without changing the Model. A new calculated series requires a saved, visible Model row.

## Give Ari useful context

Include these details when they matter:

- The target Variable
- The business calculation
- The table or page
- The Dimension or segment.
- Actuals, Forecast, or all periods
- The Scenario to use.

You do not need to provide Variable IDs or formula conditions. Ari resolves those from the model.

## Review the result

After a change, ask Ari to check the visible values, the formula's scope, and the Scenario where the result was saved.
