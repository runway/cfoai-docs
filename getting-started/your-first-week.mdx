Your first Model is a starting point. Take one short task a day for the next five days.

Each task has a prompt to paste into Ari. Replace the words in brackets with your own, and find more in [Ari prompts](/ari/prompts). Start a new chat each day with **⌘ /** (Ctrl+/ on Windows and Linux).

No Model yet? Start with [Your first 10 minutes](/getting-started/first-10-minutes). If its numbers did not match your books, [fix that first](/getting-started/first-10-minutes#if-something-looks-wrong).

<Steps>
  <Step icon="chart-line" title="Day 2: Build out your forecast">
    Build your forecast from what moves your business, so you can explain every number in it.

    > Build out our forecast. Calculate revenue from [new customers, pricing, and churn] and costs from [headcount and our biggest vendors]. Put the assumptions where I can see and change them.

    Then trace one forecast number. Right-click the cell, choose **Mention cell in chat**, and ask:

    > How is this calculated? Which assumptions move it the most?

    To explain a whole table, hover its left edge, click the six-dot handle, and choose **Explain with Ari**. Every change Ari made is in [Version history](/getting-started/build-by-hand#version-history-and-undo), the clock icon in the Page header.

    You are done when you can name the two or three assumptions that move your forecast most. Tomorrow, you turn it into something other people can read.

  </Step>
  <Step icon="palette" title="Day 3: Build a Page you would share">
    A Page is how your board, investors, or team see the Model. A Canvas block is a custom visual Ari writes for you, so you can ask for more than standard charts:

    > Build a [board / monthly] summary Page with a row of our five key numbers and their change since last month, a cash runway gauge, and a slider so I can try different hiring dates and see the effect on cash.

    For ideas, select **Browse Canvas block library** in the chat composer and pick a template.

    <Frame caption="The Canvas block library button sits at the bottom left of the chat composer.">
      <img src="/images/canvas-block-library-button.png" width="367" alt="The Ari chat composer with the palette icon at the bottom left highlighted and labeled Browse Canvas block library." />
    </Frame>

    More ideas: [Ari prompts](/ari/prompts#interactive-visuals) and [Charts and custom visualizations](/dashboards/charts-and-custom-visualizations).

    Then bring someone in:

    - To show them the Page, select **Share** in the Page header for a read-only link. It opens without a sign-in. See [Share a Page and choose its Scenario](/dashboards/share-pages-and-scenarios).
    - To build with you, ask Ari: "Invite [name@company.com] to the workspace." Invited teammates can edit the Model and chat with Ari. See [Workspace and team settings](/workspace-settings/team-and-workspace).

    You are done when you have shared the Page or invited one teammate. Tomorrow, you test a different plan.

  </Step>
  <Step icon="code-branch" title="Day 4: Test a decision in a Scenario">
    A Scenario lets you try a plan without changing Main, so you see what a decision costs before you make it.

    > Create a Scenario called [Hire two engineers in Q1]. [Add two engineers at $X per year starting in January.] Compare cash and cash runway with Main, and add the comparison to my [board] Page.

    See [Create and compare Scenarios](/scenarios/create-and-compare-scenarios).

    You are done when your Page shows what the decision does to cash runway. Tomorrow, you find where the Model is weakest.

  </Step>
  <Step icon="magnifying-glass-chart" title="Day 5: Pressure-test your Model">
    Every company is different, so ask Ari what your Model needs next:

    > Review my Model without changing it. Which assumptions look weakest compared with our history? What is missing, and what should I build next?

    Ari might suggest checking a growth rate against your actuals, adding pricing or churn detail, or [building headcount from payroll](/headcount/build-a-headcount-page). Pick the one that matters most and ask Ari to do it.

    You are done when you have fixed or built one thing Ari flagged. Tomorrow, you have Ari tell you when something changes.

  </Step>
  <Step icon="clock" title="Day 6: Let Ari keep watch">
    A Trigger runs a prompt on a schedule and sends you the result, so you hear what matters without opening the app. Start with one that runs this week:

    > Every Monday at 8am [your time zone], summarize last week's cash in and out, show our current cash runway, and flag any expense that looks different from our recent pattern. Include a link to my [board] Page.

    More ideas:

    - "Every Friday at 4pm, tell me how this month is tracking against the forecast and what to watch next week."
    - "Every Monday, tell me how cash runway changed since last week and why."
    - "On the 5th of every month, compare last month's actuals with the forecast and explain the three biggest differences."

    Ari asks whether to send results to your Slack direct messages or your email. See [Schedule recurring work with Triggers](/automation/triggers). For a process you repeat yourself, such as closing the month, teach it to Ari once as a [Skill](/automation/skills).

    You are done when you have one Trigger saved.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## After the first week

Connected sources refresh every day. Each month, after your books close:

1. Read the actuals-versus-forecast comparison, from your Trigger or by asking Ari.
2. Update the assumptions that turned out wrong.
3. Check your shared Page before it goes out.

After your first month, list what you still do by hand and ask Ari to take it over:

> Here is what I still do by hand each month: [list]. Which of these can you do as a Skill or a Trigger? Set up the first one.

## Get better answers from Ari

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Talk to Ari plainly">
    Instead of: a long prompt that spells out every table and formula.

    Try: "Build our P&L for next year from QuickBooks." Ari works out the details and asks when it needs something.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Say the decision, not the report">
    Instead of: "Build a forecast."

    Try: "Build a 12-month forecast so I can see whether we can hire two engineers in Q1."
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Point at what you mean">
    Instead of: "Why is the revenue number in that table wrong?"

    Try: right-click the cell, choose **Mention cell in chat**, and ask "Why is this lower than last month?"
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Say whether you want an answer or a change">
    Instead of: "What about gross margin?"

    Try: "Explain why gross margin fell in March. Do not change the Model." Or: "Update the gross margin formula in the Downside Scenario."
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Correct Ari when it guesses">
    Instead of: starting over in a new chat.

    Try: "Our customers pay annually up front, not monthly. Fix the revenue timing and remember this." See [What Ari remembers](/ari/what-ari-remembers).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Steer a long build">
    Instead of: waiting while a build goes the wrong way.

    Try: select **Stop**, or type a correction while Ari works. Ari reads it as soon as it finishes what it is doing. See [Steer, stop, and approve during a chat](/ari/during-a-conversation).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Undo instead of starting over">
    Instead of: deleting a Page when a change goes wrong.

    Try: open [Version history](/getting-started/build-by-hand#version-history-and-undo) and select **Undo this change** or **Restore this version**, or ask Ari "Undo that last change."
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

See [Ask Ari better questions](/ari/ask-better-questions) for more.
