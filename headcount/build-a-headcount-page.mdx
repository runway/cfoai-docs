Once an HR or payroll system such as Rippling is connected, or an employee roster is uploaded as a CSV, ask Ari to build a **Headcount page**. Ari builds two tables from the same records, with the formulas behind them, so the page answers who is employed each month and what they cost.

## What Ari needs

Ari reads the connection's fields and resolves them to the Variables and Dimensions the page needs:

- **Employee ID** — the identity of each person. Two people with the same name stay two people.
- **Start date** and **termination date** — who is employed in each month. An empty termination date means the employment is still open.
- **Pay** and how it is paid — annual, monthly, or hourly. Hourly pay needs an hours assumption, which Ari keeps as an editable input.
- **Department** — how the monthly table rolls people up.

Job title, employment type, location, pay currency, and other attributes come along when the source has them.

## What you get

- A **roster**: a record table with one row per Employee ID and each attribute in its own column. It shows each person's latest values.
- A **monthly table**: headcount and payroll cost by period, rolled up by department. Each month reads that month's own records, so a raise or a department transfer shows in the month it happened.

Ask Ari to name the new Page, or accept the default. From there, treat it like any other Page: ask Ari to add comparisons, reformat it, or build a Scenario to model a hiring plan against it.

## How Ari counts and pays

- A month's headcount counts anyone employed at any point in the month, so the start month and the termination month both count. If your reporting uses another convention, such as month-end headcount, say so and Ari applies it.
- Annual pay divided by twelve is a full month at unchanged pay, and a monthly rate is a month's pay as it stands. Partial months follow the employment dates.
- Loaded cost such as taxes and benefits comes from the records when they carry it, and otherwise from an assumption Ari states in the Model, where you can edit it.
- Hires, departures, and attrition that are not in the records are planning assumptions. Ari keeps them as editable inputs, separate from the people in the source.

## Before you plan against it

**Last close** separates Actuals from Forecast in every table, including this one. Set it before you plan hiring against the page so the months in your books read the records and the later months read your plan. See [Actuals, Forecast, and formula ranges](/formulas/core-concepts/actuals-forecast-and-formula-ranges).
