## Accounting systems

cfo.ai connects directly to the accounting systems most finance teams already use:

- **NetSuite** — imports Actuals for revenue, expenses, and balance sheet accounts, including reconciliation against voided or deleted transactions to keep historical Actuals accurate.
- **QuickBooks Online (QBO)** — imports Actuals for smaller or single-entity businesses, including class and location dimensions where available.
- **Xero** — imports Actuals for businesses on Xero, including multi-currency accounts.

Use an accounting system integration when you want Actuals to update automatically each time your books close.

## Data warehouses and pipelines

For teams with data already centralized in a warehouse, cfo.ai can connect through **Fivetran** to sources such as **Snowflake**, bringing in general ledger detail, subledger data, or operational data that lives outside your accounting system.

Use a warehouse integration when your data comes from more than one system, needs custom transformation before it reaches cfo.ai, or already flows through an existing pipeline.

## Multi-entity businesses

If your business has more than one legal entity, cfo.ai can bring in Actuals for each entity separately and combine them into a consolidated view, so entity-level detail and consolidated reporting stay in sync.

## Keeping data current

Once an integration is connected, cfo.ai syncs on a regular schedule so Actuals reflect your latest close without a manual import.

## Something look off?

A mismatch between your source system and cfo.ai is usually a sync timing issue, a mapping difference, or a stale transaction that has not been reconciled yet.
