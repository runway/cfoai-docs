Your workspace has a few settings that apply model-wide, rather than to any single Variable or Table Block. Ask Ari to change most of these directly.

## Rename the workspace

Ask Ari to rename your workspace. Renaming regenerates the workspace's URL slug, so any link built from the old slug changes along with it.

## Time defaults

Ask Ari to set the model's default time settings, or open **Settings → General** and use the **Date & Time** section yourself:

- **Default date granularity** — the default roll-up grain for new tables. Month unless you change it.
- **Default date range** — the default start and end date a new table shows.
- **Last close** — described below.

These are defaults for new Table Blocks; an existing table's own window (see [Build and format a Table Block](/dashboards/build-and-format-tables)) isn't affected until you change it directly.

## Last close

**Last close** is the date your Model computes Actuals against — everything at or before it is Actuals, and everything after is Forecast. Ask Ari to set it after each period closes in your books. Several other workflows, like [building a Headcount page](/headcount/build-a-headcount-page), depend on Last close being set correctly first.

## Team directory

Ask Ari to look up who is in the workspace, including current team members and pending invitations. Every member of a workspace is an administrator; there is no lower workspace role and there are no user groups. Email addresses are shown only to people with workspace-wide access-control permission. People who opened a Page through an external link are counted but not identified.

To give someone a report without making them an administrator, share the Page with an external link instead; see [Share a Page and choose its Scenario](/dashboards/share-pages-and-scenarios).

## Invite or remove workspace administrators

There is no team screen in Settings. Ask Ari, and when you have the required access it can:

- **Invite** someone as a workspace administrator. They receive an email invitation. Inviting an address whose invitation is still pending sends the email again, so ask Ari to resend rather than to cancel and re-invite.
- **Promote** an existing member to administrator. This changes their role and sends no invitation.
- **Remove** a member who has joined. Ari refuses to remove you or the workspace's last administrator.
- **Cancel** a pending invitation. Cancelling and removing are separate actions: Ari refuses to cancel an invitation for someone who has already joined, and that person keeps their access, so a colleague who accepts while you are talking to Ari is never removed by mistake.

> Invite dana@example.com to the workspace.

> Who has a pending invitation, and can you cancel the one for the old contractor?

Ari cannot invite someone with a lower role or revoke a share link. Ari can create a view-only share link to a Page; see [Share a Page and choose its Scenario](/dashboards/share-pages-and-scenarios).

## Delete a workspace or an account

There is no self-serve control for deleting a workspace or a personal account. Settings has no such action, and Ari cannot do it either. Removing a member takes away that person's access to the workspace without deleting their account.

To ask for a workspace or an account and its data to be deleted, select **Support** in the navigation at the top of these docs and name the workspace you mean. Canceling a plan is a separate step: it stops the billing and leaves the data in place. See [Billing and plan](/workspace-settings/billing).
