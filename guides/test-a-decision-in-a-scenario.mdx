title: "Test a decision in a Scenario"

---

Use a Scenario when you want to see what a decision does before you make it, such as two new hires, a price increase, or a hiring freeze. A Scenario is a what-if version of your plan. It starts as a copy of Main, the Scenario that holds your current plan, and keeps receiving Main's updates. Your changes stay in the Scenario until you merge them. To understand how that works, see [Scenarios, Snapshots, and version history](/concepts/scenarios).

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Ask Ari">
    Name the Scenario and describe the decision and what you want to see:

    > Create a Scenario called Hire two engineers in Q1. Add two engineers at \$150,000 a year each starting in January, and compare cash and cash runway with Main.

    Ari creates the Scenario, makes the changes in it, and shows the comparison. Main is not changed. Other decisions work the same way:

    > Can we hire three engineers in September? Build a hiring Scenario and show the effect on payroll, cash, and cash runway.

    > Create a Scenario where enterprise churn falls by two percentage points and show the effect on revenue and cash over the next year.

    To keep working in the Scenario later, open it from the Scenario picker, or name it in your request:

    > In the Hire two engineers in Q1 Scenario, move the start date to March.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="By hand">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Create the Scenario">
        Open the Scenario picker, the **Main Scenario** control in the sidebar's **Model** section, above your Pages. Select **Create scenario**, type a name, and press **Enter**. cfo.ai creates the Scenario and switches to it.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Change the assumptions">
        Edit the values and formulas the decision touches, on any Page. Every edit you make while the Scenario is selected is saved in the Scenario, not in Main.
      </Step>
      <Step title="See the effect">
        Read the Pages in the Scenario, or switch back to Main in the picker to see the plan without your changes. To see both versions on one table, [compare Scenarios side by side](/guides/compare-scenarios).
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Creating a Scenario needs permission to create Scenarios, and it is not offered on a phone.

## Start from a Scenario you already built

To try a second version of a plan, start from that plan instead of from Main. The new Scenario begins with everything the original changed and then goes its own way; see [A copy starts where another Scenario is](/concepts/scenarios#a-copy-starts-where-another-scenario-is).

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Ask Ari">
    > Create a Scenario called Hire two engineers, later from the Hire two engineers in Q1 Scenario, and move both start dates to April.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="By hand">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the Scenario you want to copy">
        Select it in the Scenario picker.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Duplicate it">
        Open the picker again and select **Duplicate scenario**. cfo.ai creates **[Scenario] copy** and switches to it.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Rename the copy">
        Point at the copy's row in the picker, select its gear icon, **Open actions for [Scenario]**, and choose **Rename**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Duplicating a Snapshot gives you an editable Scenario on Main that starts from the Snapshot's contents; see [Save a plan as a Snapshot](/guides/save-a-snapshot#work-from-a-snapshot). Main has no **Duplicate scenario**; use **Create scenario** there.

## If you cannot find your changes

Check the Scenario picker. Pages show the Scenario selected there. Changes you made in a Scenario, including new Pages, appear only while that Scenario is selected, until you merge it. The address bar changes when you switch Scenarios, so you can bookmark a Scenario or send a teammate a link to it.
