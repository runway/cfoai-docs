Every change to the Model is recorded in version history, whether you made it or Ari did, so you can take any of them back. Choose the method by whose change it is and how far back you want to go.

| You want to | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Take back an edit you just made | Press **⌘ Z** (**Ctrl Z** on Windows and Linux) |
| Take back a change Ari made | Ask Ari, or undo the entry in version history |
| Take back one older change and keep everything after it | Undo that entry in version history |
| Return the whole Scenario to how it was at a point in time | Restore that version |

## Undo your own last edit

Press **⌘ Z** (**Ctrl Z** on Windows and Linux) to take back the last change you made to the Model, and **⌘ Shift Z** to redo it.

**⌘ Z** undoes only the edits you made in this browser tab since it opened. It does not undo Ari's changes or changes from an earlier visit; use version history for those. It does not undo Model changes while you are typing in a text field or the formula editor. The notices undo and redo show are listed in [Keyboard shortcuts](/reference/keyboard-shortcuts#undo-and-redo).

## Ask Ari to undo

> Undo that last change.

Ari undoes the most recent change as a whole. You can also describe an older change, such as "Undo the change to the hiring plan from this morning." Ari reads the history and undoes the change you meant.

## Undo one change in version history

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open version history">
    Select **Version history**, the clock icon in the Page header. Entries are listed newest first, each with who made the change, when, and what it touched.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the change">
    Use **Filter version history** to show only some people or kinds of change, or **Jump to date** to move to a day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Undo it">
    Point at the entry, select its **…** actions button, and choose **Undo this change**. Undo puts back only what that change wrote, such as a Page's name or one formula, and keeps every later change. If a later change edited the same thing, nothing is undone, and a notice names that later change: **A later change edited the [field] of [subject], so this change can’t be undone on its own.** Undo the later change first, then this one. A change a Scenario inherited from Main cannot be undone from inside the Scenario; undo it in Main.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An undone entry offers **Redo this change**.

## Restore an earlier version

Restoring returns the whole Scenario to an earlier point and rolls back every change made after it.

1. In version history, select the entry for the point you want. The Model shows how it looked then, with a banner that says you are viewing a version.
2. Select **Restore this version**, or open the entry's **…** actions button and choose **Restore this version**.
3. Review the preview and confirm.

The restore is itself a recorded change, so you can undo it. Restoring needs full access to the Scenario. In a Scenario other than Main, changes made in Main stay as they are.

To leave the past version without restoring it, select **Back to latest**.
