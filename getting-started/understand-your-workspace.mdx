Your workspace is your company's home in cfo.ai. Everything you build or connect belongs to that workspace, including your Model, reporting Pages, Scenarios, integrations, team, and saved workflows.

## One Model, many useful views

A Model represents how your business operates across time. Its Variables hold the values you care about, its Dimensions explain how those values are organized, and its formulas connect assumptions, source data, and financial results.

A Page presents a useful part of that Model. One Page might show a profit and loss statement, another might show headcount, and a third might present a board report. The Pages can refer to the same Revenue, Department, or Cash values without creating separate copies of them.

## The main parts of your workspace

- **Pages** organize tables, charts, and written context in the sidebar.
- **Table Blocks** show live Model values across time and business categories.
- **Scenarios** let you compare an alternative plan with Main.
- **Integrations** connect the systems that hold your financial and operating data.
- **Ari** answers questions, explains results, and builds or changes Model content when you ask.
- **Settings** hold workspace details, team access, billing, connections, and personal preferences.

## Work from the current Page

When you ask Ari about the Page you have open, Ari can use that Page as context. Point to a table, row, or cell when you want an explanation tied to a particular number.

> Why is payroll higher in April on this Page?

> Add a chart showing the last 12 months of revenue above this table.

> Create a Scenario that assumes we hire two account executives in June.

## Switch context when it matters

If you have access to more than one workspace, use the workspace switcher to choose the right company or entity. Within a workspace, name the Scenario when a question or change should apply somewhere other than the one you are viewing.
