cfo.ai connects to the systems where your financial and operational data already lives, so your Model stays current without manual entry.

<Frame>
  ![Integrations directory in cfo.ai](/images/integrations.png)
</Frame>

## File upload

**CSV / file upload** is a manual option for bringing in a raw file directly. Use it when a live connection is not set up yet, or for a one-off or specialized report a system does not sync automatically.

## Accounting and ERP

- **QuickBooks Online**
- **Xero**
- **NetSuite SuiteAnalytics** (via Fivetran)

Use an accounting or ERP integration when you want Actuals to update automatically each time your books close.

## HR

- **Rippling** (via Fivetran)

Connect Rippling to bring headcount, compensation, and other people data into your Model alongside your financials.

## Spreadsheets

- **Google Sheets** (via Fivetran)

Use a Google Sheets connection when a Variable or driver is still maintained in a spreadsheet outside your other systems.

## Databases

- **PostgreSQL** (via Fivetran)
- **Amazon RDS for PostgreSQL** (via Fivetran)
- **Amazon Aurora PostgreSQL** (via Fivetran)
- **Google Cloud SQL for PostgreSQL** (via Fivetran)

Use a database connection when your data lives in a system of record that does not have a dedicated integration above, but is reachable through one of these PostgreSQL-compatible sources.

## About Fivetran

Most connectors other than CSV upload, QuickBooks Online, and Xero run through **Fivetran**, which handles the underlying sync. You connect the source once, and Fivetran keeps it up to date on a regular schedule.
