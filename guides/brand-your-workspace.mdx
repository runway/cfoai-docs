Brand your workspace when you want cfo.ai, and the Pages you share, to show your company's look: its name, logo, colors, heading typeface, and corner shape. Everyone who opens the workspace sees the brand, and so does anyone who opens a Page you share. The fastest way to get one is to point Ari at your company's website.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Ask Ari">
    Name your website:

    > Make this workspace look like yourfirm.com.

    Ari reads the site's home page and stylesheets. It picks the accent color, the nearest heading typeface it offers, the corner shape, and the logo, then saves the brand. It chooses colors for the mode the site does not show, so the brand works in both light and dark mode. Your open app switches to the brand at once.

    Ari then lists what it read from the site, what it chose, and any color cfo.ai adjusted to keep text readable. If Ari cannot reach the site, it asks you for the colors and a logo file.

    You can also give Ari the brand directly: attach your logo and say which colors and typeface to use. A logo must be a PNG, JPEG, or WebP image of 1 MB or less and at most 2,000 pixels wide or tall. If your site's logo is an SVG file, Ari converts it to PNG or asks you for an image file.

    In **Settings → General → Brand**, typing your site in **Website address** and selecting **Build from a website** starts the same chat.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="By hand">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the Brand section">
        Open the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar, select **Settings**, and on the **General** tab find **Brand**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Start the brand">
        Select **Set up by hand**. The form starts from your own theme's colors and the workspace's name.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Fill in the brand">
        Set the **Name**, upload a **Logo** and, if the first one is hard to see on a dark background, a **Logo for dark mode**. For **Light mode** and **Dark mode**, enter the **Page surface**, **Panel surface**, **Text**, and **Accent** colors as hex or OKLCH values. Pick a **Heading typeface** and a **Corner shape**. The preview beside the form shows the result.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Save">
        Select **Save**. cfo.ai moves any color that would make text hard to read to the nearest readable color.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## What a brand contains

| Part | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Name | 1 to 40 characters, usually your company's name. Readers of shared Pages see it. |
| Logo | Optional. One image for light mode and an optional second one for dark mode. |
| Colors | For each of light and dark mode: the page surface, the panel surface, the text, and the accent, which marks selections, focus, and the highlights in Canvas blocks |
| Heading typeface | Optional. **cfo.ai default**, **Source Serif**, **Newsreader**, **Fraunces**, **Zilla Slab**, or **DM Sans**. It sets Page titles, headings, and block titles; body text and numbers keep cfo.ai's own fonts. |
| Corner shape | **Square**, **Soft** (cfo.ai's own), or **Round** |
| Chart colors | Optional, through Ari: up to three colors that replace the first chart series colors |

## Who sees the brand

Everyone who opens the workspace sees the brand. Pages, tables, charts, and Canvas blocks all take its look with no change to any of them. The sidebar shows your logo, or your name's first letter when there is no logo, and a **Powered by cfo.ai** line. Each person still chooses light, dark, or automatic mode for themselves.

People who open a shared Page see it too. A reader who follows a share link sees the Page in your brand, under a strip with your logo, your name, and **Powered by cfo.ai**. The link's preview card in chat and social apps does not name your brand. See [Sharing and access](/concepts/sharing-and-access).

Documents Ari makes for you, such as a PDF report or a deck, use your brand's colors, heading typeface, and logo. See [Get a PDF report or slide deck](/guides/get-a-pdf-or-slides).

Any administrator can set or change the brand, and everyone in a workspace is an administrator.

## Change, copy, or remove the brand

To change the brand, ask Ari, such as "Use a darker accent color", or select **Edit** in **Settings → General → Brand**. A change replaces the brand as a whole, and everyone sees it at once. To go back to the earlier brand, ask Ari in the same chat to put it back; Ari keeps the brand it replaced.

To use the brand in your other workspaces, select **Copy to other workspaces** in the Brand section, tick the workspaces, and select the copy button. Or ask Ari, in the other workspace, to copy the brand from this one. Each copy is separate: a later change to one workspace's brand leaves the others as they are.

To remove the brand, select **Remove brand** and confirm, or ask Ari to remove it. Everyone sees their own theme again, and shared Pages lose your name and logo. Copies in other workspaces stay.

## A brand and your personal theme

A brand belongs to the workspace and everyone sees it. A theme belongs to you and changes only what you see. In a workspace with a brand, the brand replaces your theme, and **Settings → General → Appearance** says which brand the workspace uses. Your own theme still applies in your workspaces that have no brand. To set your theme, see [Change your theme, sounds, and notifications](/guides/personalize-cfoai).

Every label and message in the Brand section is listed in [Settings](/reference/settings).
