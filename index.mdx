Ari is your AI finance coworker. Ask whether you can afford to hire, why spending increased, or what happens if growth slows. Ari can answer the question and build or update the shared Model behind it.

You can open that Model, inspect the assumptions, change them, and see how the answer changes. The work remains available after the conversation ends.

> Can we hire three engineers in September? Show the effect on payroll, cash, and cash runway.
