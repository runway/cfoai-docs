Your first Model is a starting point. Over the next five days, take one short task a day. Each task teaches you one part of cfo.ai and leaves your Model more accurate than it was.

Each task has a prompt you can paste into Ari, and [Ari prompts](/ari/prompts) has more. Replace the words in brackets with your own. Start a new chat for each day with **⌘ /** (Ctrl+/ on Windows and Linux) so each conversation stays about one task.

If you have not built a first Model yet, start with [Your first 10 minutes](/getting-started/first-10-minutes).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Day 2: Check your actuals against your books">
    **Why:** you can only trust a forecast that starts from the right history.

    > Compare [last month]'s revenue, expenses, and net income in the Model with [QuickBooks / my P&L]. List every account that differs and explain why.

    If you imported a workbook instead, attach it to this new chat again and ask:

    > Reconcile the rebuilt revenue, payroll, and cash totals to this workbook for every quarter. Explain any difference you cannot resolve.

    **Done when:** the totals match, or every gap has a reason you agree with.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Day 3: Trace a number to its source">
    **Why:** knowing where a number comes from is how you explain it to your team or board.

    - For one number, select the cell, press **⌘ L** (Ctrl+L), and ask: "Where does this come from? Show the formula and the source data."
    - For a whole table, hover the table's left edge, click the six-dot handle, and choose **Explain with Ari**.
    - To see every change, who made it, and when, open **Version history**, the clock icon in the Page header. You can undo any one change or restore an earlier version from there.

    **Done when:** you can say in one sentence how your largest revenue or cost number is calculated.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Day 4: Test a decision in a Scenario">
    **Why:** a Scenario lets you try a plan without changing your main Model.

    > Create a Scenario called [Hire two engineers in Q1]. [Add two engineers at $X per year starting in January.] Compare cash and cash runway with Main.

    **Done when:** you can see the Scenario next to Main and know what the decision costs.

    See [Create and compare Scenarios](/scenarios/create-and-compare-scenarios).

  </Step>
  <Step title="Day 5: Build a Page you would share">
    **Why:** a Page is how the Model reaches the people who use it, such as your board, investors, or team.

    > Build a [board / monthly] summary Page with revenue, gross margin, cash, cash runway, and headcount. Add a chart for [revenue vs. plan] and a short note on the biggest changes since last month.

    When it looks right, select **Share** in the Page header to create a read-only link. See [Share a Page and choose its Scenario](/dashboards/share-pages-and-scenarios).

    **Done when:** you would send this Page to someone without editing it first.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Day 6: Make it repeat">
    **Why:** the Model keeps working when you are not in it.

    > Every Monday at 8am [your time zone], compare last week's spending with the forecast, flag anything more than 10% off, and send it to my email.

    Ari asks where to deliver the result, your Slack direct messages or your email, before it saves the Trigger. See [Schedule recurring work with Triggers](/automation/triggers).

    If you have a process you repeat yourself, such as how you close the month, you can teach it to Ari once as a [Skill](/automation/skills).

    **Done when:** you have one Trigger or Skill saved.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## After the first week

Each month, after your books close:

1. Ask Ari to refresh your connections and confirm the new month arrived.
2. Ask Ari to compare actuals with the forecast and explain the largest differences.
3. Update the assumptions that turned out wrong.
4. Open your shared Page and check it before it goes out.

After your first month, write down what you still do by hand, such as copying numbers into a deck or rebuilding the same table. Ask Ari to take each one over:

> Here is what I still do by hand each month: [list]. Which of these can you do for me as a Skill or a Trigger? Set up the first one.

## Get better answers from Ari

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Talk to Ari plainly">
    Instead of: a long, detailed prompt that spells out every table and formula.

    Try: "Build our P&L for next year from QuickBooks." Ari works out the details and asks when it needs something from you.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Say the decision, not the report">
    Instead of: "Build a forecast."

    Try: "Build a 12-month forecast so I can see whether we can hire two engineers in Q1."
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Point at what you mean">
    Instead of: "Why is the revenue number in that table wrong?"

    Try: select the cell, press **⌘ L** (Ctrl+L), and ask "Why is this lower than last month?"
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Say whether you want an answer or a change">
    Instead of: "What about gross margin?"

    Try: "Explain why gross margin fell in March. Do not change the Model." Or: "Update the gross margin formula in the Downside Scenario."
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Correct Ari when it guesses">
    Instead of: starting over in a new chat.

    Try: "Our customers pay annually up front, not monthly. Fix the revenue timing and remember this." See [What Ari remembers](/ari/what-ari-remembers).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Steer a long build">
    Instead of: waiting for a build that is going the wrong way.

    Try: select **Stop**, or type a correction while Ari works; Ari reads it as soon as it finishes what it is doing. See [Steer, stop, and approve during a chat](/ari/during-a-conversation).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Undo instead of starting over">
    Instead of: deleting a Page or opening a new workspace when a change goes wrong.

    Try: open **Version history** and select **Undo this change** or **Restore this version**, or ask Ari "Undo that last change."
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

For more, see [Ask Ari better questions](/ari/ask-better-questions).
