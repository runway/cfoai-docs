Use a comparison when you want to see two versions of your plan, such as Main and a Scenario, or this period against an earlier one, on the same table. A comparison is saved with the table, so everyone who opens the Page sees it, including readers of a share link.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Ask Ari">
    > Add a comparison to the cash table showing the Hiring freeze Scenario beside Main, with the variance in dollars and percent.

    Or create the Scenario and compare in one request:

    > Create a Hiring freeze Scenario, pause new hires through December, and compare payroll and ending cash with Main.

    To read values that already exist without changing any table:

    > How does Operating Expense in the Hiring freeze Scenario compare with Main for next quarter? Don't change anything.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="By hand">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the comparison settings">
        Select **Compare**, the two-arrows button in the table's header. It is not shown on a phone or to anyone who cannot edit the table.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose what to compare">
        Under **Compare between**, choose **Scenarios** and pick the Scenarios to show beside the one you are viewing. Or choose **Time periods** to compare each period with an earlier one, and pick the earlier period, such as the previous period or a set number of periods ago.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose the measures">
        Under **Variables**, turn on one or more of the available measures: **Value**, **Variance**, and **Variance %**; a time comparison also offers **Comparison value**. At least one stays on. A Scenario comparison starts with **Value** only, and a time comparison starts with **Value** and **Comparison value**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose the layout">
        Under **Display as**, show the comparisons in **Columns** or **Rows**. For a Scenario comparison in **Rows**, **Group by** orders them **By scenario** or **By variable**; this grouping is a view setting and is not saved with the table.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    To remove the comparison, open **Compare** and select **Reset**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

A table compares either Scenarios or time periods, not both at once. Comparing time periods needs exactly one Date row or column on the table.

## How Variance and Variance % are calculated

In a Scenario comparison, **Variance** is the compared Scenario's value minus the value in the Scenario you are viewing. **Variance %** is that difference divided by the viewed value, and is blank when the viewed value is zero or empty. In a time comparison, **Variance** is the current period minus the earlier one. Whether a positive variance shows as good or bad depends on the Variable's **Variance colors** setting, **Higher is better** or **Lower is better**. Change it on the Variable, and every table that shows it follows; see [Format and arrange a table](/guides/format-a-table).
