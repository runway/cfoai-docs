Use a comparison when you want to see two versions of your plan, such as Main and a Scenario, or this period against an earlier one, on the same table. A comparison is saved with the table, so everyone who opens the Page sees it, including readers of a share link.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Ask Ari">
    > Add a comparison to the cash table showing the Hiring freeze Scenario beside Main, with the variance in dollars and percent.

    Or create the Scenario and compare in one request:

    > Create a Hiring freeze Scenario, pause new hires through December, and compare payroll and ending cash with Main.

    To read values that already exist without changing any table:

    > How does Operating Expense in the Hiring freeze Scenario compare with Main for next quarter? Don't change anything.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="By hand">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the comparison settings">
        Select **Compare**, the two-arrows button in the table's header. It is not shown on a phone or to anyone who cannot edit the table.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose what to compare">
        Under **Compare between**, choose **Scenarios** and pick the Scenarios to show beside the one you are viewing. Or choose **Time periods** to compare each period with an earlier one, and pick the earlier period, such as the previous period or a set number of periods ago. **None** removes the comparison, as **Reset** does. A time comparison needs exactly one Date row or column; the message the panel shows otherwise is in [Table block controls and messages](/reference/table-blocks#table-header).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose the measures">
        Under **Variables**, turn on one or more of the available measures: **Value**, **Variance**, and **Variance %**; a time comparison also offers **Comparison value**. At least one stays on. A Scenario comparison starts with **Value** only, and a time comparison starts with **Value** and **Comparison value**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose the layout">
        Under **Display as**, show the comparisons in **Columns** or **Rows**. For a Scenario comparison in **Rows**, **Group by** orders them **By scenario** or **By variable**; this grouping is a view setting and is not saved with the table.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    To remove the comparison, open **Compare** and select **Reset**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

A table compares either Scenarios or time periods, not both at once. Comparing time periods needs exactly one Date row or column on the table.

## Keep a table on one Scenario

To show two Scenarios in separate tables on one Page, such as a Plan table above a Plan+ table, choose a Scenario for each table. A table with a Scenario chosen shows that Scenario whatever Scenario the Page is open in. A comparison cannot do this: it adds columns beside the Scenario the table already shows.

You need a desktop browser, edit access to the Page, and access to the Scenario you choose.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Ask Ari">
    > On the Board summary Page, make the Plan revenue table always show the Plan Scenario and the Plan+ revenue table always show Plan+.

    To undo it:

    > Make the Plan revenue table and the Plan+ revenue table show the Page's Scenario again.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="By hand">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the table's Scenario menu">
        Select the branch icon at the right end of the table's header, after **Table block settings**. Its tooltip reads **Scenario this table shows**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose the Scenario">
        Choose a Scenario or Snapshot from the list. The header shows its name in place of the icon, and the table shows that Scenario's or Snapshot's rows, values, and formulas.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    To make the table follow its Page again, select the Scenario's name in the header and choose **The page’s scenario**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The list holds your Snapshots as well as your Scenarios, and a table set to a Snapshot follows the same rules. A value, formula, row, column, or format you change in such a table saves in the Scenario or Snapshot it shows, not in the Scenario the Page is open in. The choice itself is saved with the table in the Page's Scenario. A reader who cannot open the chosen Scenario or Snapshot sees a note in place of the table's values. A spreadsheet download writes the table with the chosen Scenario's or Snapshot's numbers, or leaves the table out when the person downloading cannot open it. The rules and messages are in [A table that always shows one Scenario](/reference/table-blocks#a-table-that-always-shows-one-scenario).

## How Variance and Variance % are calculated

In a Scenario comparison, **Variance** is the compared Scenario's value minus the value in the Scenario you are viewing. **Variance %** is that difference divided by the viewed value, and is blank when the viewed value is zero or empty. In a time comparison, **Variance** is the current period minus the earlier one, and **Variance %** is that difference divided by the earlier period's value. It is blank when the earlier value is zero or empty. Whether a positive variance shows as good or bad depends on the Variable's **Variance colors** setting, **Higher is better** or **Lower is better**. Change it on the Variable, and every table that shows it follows; see [Format and arrange a table](/guides/format-a-table).
