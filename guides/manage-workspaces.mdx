Create another workspace to keep a separate business apart, such as a second venture, or one company per client if you are a fractional CFO or an advisor. Each workspace is separate: it has its own Model, Pages, connections, chats, Triggers, team, and plan. Your Skills are yours and go with you to every workspace where you are a member, unless you limit a Skill to one workspace; see [Save a workflow as a Skill](/guides/save-a-skill).

To report several legal entities of one business together, such as a US company and its UK subsidiary, keep them in one workspace instead; see [Model several entities and consolidate them](/guides/consolidate-entities).

## Create a workspace

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Ask Ari">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Ask in a chat in the cfo.ai web app">
        Say that you want a separate workspace, give it a name, and say what you want done there:

        > Create a separate workspace called Client Co and build a 12-month cash forecast there from their QuickBooks.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open the workspace from the card">
        Ari creates the workspace and shows a card with its name in the chat. Select the card to open the workspace.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose a plan">
        cfo.ai asks you to choose a plan for the new workspace.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    When setup is finished, the workspace opens on a chat named **Set up** followed by the workspace's name, and Ari starts there on the task you gave it. The workspace you asked from does not change. For the card, and for where Ari will not create a workspace, see [A workspace Ari creates](/reference/chat#a-workspace-ari-creates).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="By hand">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the account menu">
        Select your name or picture at the bottom of the sidebar.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose Create workspace">
        Under **Workspaces**, select **Create workspace**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Name it">
        Type the **Workspace name** and press **Enter**, or select **Create** followed by the name.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    Before the new workspace opens, cfo.ai asks you to choose a plan for it. The workspace then opens on its own Welcome chat, the same start a new account gets.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Each new workspace can start its own free trial; see [Start a trial or choose a plan](/guides/choose-a-plan). Creating a workspace starts no plan and charges nothing, whether you or Ari creates it. You are its only member, as an administrator, until you invite someone.

Creating a workspace needs an account with a work email address. An account with a personal address, such as a Gmail address that a teammate invited, can join workspaces and cannot create one; the field answers **Use a work email to create a workspace.** The same rule applies when you ask Ari.

## Switch between workspaces

Open the account menu. **Workspaces** lists every workspace you belong to, with a check beside the one that is open. Select another to open it.

You can also press **⌘ K** (**Ctrl K** on Windows and Linux), type part of the workspace's name, and choose it under **Workspaces** in the search results.

Workspaces have no folders or groups; the account menu lists all of them by name. Give each one a name you can tell apart at a glance.

When you sign in, or open cfo.ai without a link to a workspace, cfo.ai opens the first workspace you joined that can open. It passes over a workspace that is still waiting for its first plan, is [locked](/reference/plans-and-billing#when-the-workspace-locks), is paused while cfo.ai investigates an issue, or is deactivated, when you are a member of another workspace that can open. When none of your workspaces can open, cfo.ai opens the one you joined first, which shows its plan page or its locked screen. If you still land on a plan page you did not expect, the page offers your other workspaces under its subtitle; see [Start a trial or choose a plan](/guides/choose-a-plan#start-your-free-trial).

## Start a task in another workspace without switching

When you belong to several workspaces, you can ask Ari in one of them to start work in another. This suits an advisor who works from their firm's workspace and keeps one workspace per client.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask in a chat in the cfo.ai web app">
    Name the workspace and say what you want done there:

    > In Client Co, review October's close and list the accounts that moved more than 10%.

    If you are not sure of the name, ask Ari which other workspaces you have. Ari lists each one with your role there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the task">
    Ari starts a new chat in that workspace with your task and replies with a link to it. Select the link to follow the work.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The work happens in the other workspace, in a chat of yours there, and the answer stays in that chat. Asking twice starts two chats. For what Ari can reach in another workspace, see [What Ari can see](/concepts/working-with-ari#what-ari-can-see).

If Ari says it cannot start the task, see [A task Ari starts in another workspace](/reference/chat#a-task-ari-starts-in-another-workspace) for the reasons.

## Rename a workspace

Open **Settings**, select **General**, type the new **Name** under **Workspace**, and select **Save**. You can also ask Ari to rename it.

Renaming changes the workspace's web address. Bookmarks and links to its Pages and chats that use the old address stop working; share links keep working.

## How many workspaces a plan covers

Each workspace has its own plan and its own bill, and creating a workspace does not change the plan of the ones you have. In the plan chooser, the Starter plan card lists **1 dedicated workspace**, and the Enterprise card lists **Multiple workspaces**. To ask about Enterprise, select **Talk to sales** on its card. See [Plans and billing](/reference/plans-and-billing).

## Set up one workspace per client

If you advise several companies, give each client a workspace of its own, so their books, Models, and people never mix:

1. [Create a workspace](#create-a-workspace) and name it after the client, by asking Ari or by hand.
2. Choose a plan for it. Each workspace is billed on its own, to the payment method added in that workspace.
3. Bring in the client's books: [connect their accounting system](/guides/connect-accounting), or invite the person who holds admin access to their books and ask them to connect it.
4. Save the workflows you repeat for every client, such as your monthly close review, as a Skill once. Your Skills are yours, so Ari follows them in every workspace where you are a member, and the client's team does not get them unless you [give a teammate a copy](/guides/save-a-skill#give-a-teammate-a-copy-of-a-skill) or [make a Skill available to everyone in their workspace](/guides/save-a-skill#make-a-skill-available-to-everyone-in-a-workspace). Limit a Skill to one client's workspace when it is built on that client's own accounts, systems, people, or figures. Triggers belong to one workspace. See [Save a workflow as a Skill](/guides/save-a-skill).
5. Decide how the client sees your work:
   - To give the client full access, [invite them](/guides/invite-teammates) as an administrator, who can change anything, including billing.
   - To let the client read every Page and change nothing, invite them as a viewer.
   - To deliver reports read-only, [share Pages with a link](/guides/share-a-page) or [download a Page as a spreadsheet](/guides/download-a-spreadsheet).
6. To show your firm's or the client's look on the Pages you share, [brand the workspace](/guides/brand-your-workspace). A brand you set up once can be copied to your other workspaces.
