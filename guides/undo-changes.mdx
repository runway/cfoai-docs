Every change to the Model is recorded in version history, whether you made it or Ari did, so you can take any of them back. Choose the method by whose change it is and how far back you want to go.

| You want to | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Take back the last edit you made | Press **⌘ Z** (**Ctrl Z** on Windows and Linux) |
| Take back a change Ari made | Ask Ari, or undo the entry in version history |
| Take back one older change and keep everything after it | Undo that entry in version history |
| Return the whole Scenario to how it was at a point in time | Ask Ari, or restore that version in version history |

## Undo your own last edit

Press **⌘ Z** (**Ctrl Z** on Windows and Linux) to take back the last change you made to the Model, and **⌘ Shift Z** (**Ctrl Shift Z** or **Ctrl Y** on Windows and Linux) to redo it.

**⌘ Z** undoes only the edits you made in this browser tab since it opened. It does not undo Ari's changes or changes from an earlier visit; use version history for those. It does not undo Model changes while you are typing in a text field or the formula editor, or while you are viewing a past version; inside a Page's text, it undoes your typing instead. The notices undo and redo show are listed in [Keyboard shortcuts](/reference/keyboard-shortcuts#undo-and-redo).

## Ask Ari to undo

> Undo that last change.

Ari reads version history and undoes the entries that made up the change. Entries Ari undoes together are all undone or none are. You can also describe an older change, such as "Undo the change to the hiring plan from this morning." To bring a change back, ask Ari to redo it. Ari can undo a change only in a Scenario you can edit.

## Undo one change in version history

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open version history">
    Select **Version history**, the clock icon in the Page header. Entries are listed newest first, each with who made the change, when, and what it touched. A phone or tablet browser does not show the button; ask Ari to undo the change, or ask your browser for its desktop site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the change">
    Use **Filter version history**, the filter icon at the top of the panel, to show only some people or kinds of change, or **Jump to date**, the calendar icon beside it, to move to a day. To see what an entry changed before you undo it, point at the entry and select the arrow that appears at its left edge (**Expand change details**). An entry for a change Ari made names who asked for it, and its details show the message that asked to that person and to administrators; see [The message that asked Ari](/reference/scenarios-and-history#the-message-that-asked-ari).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Undo it">
    Point at the entry, select its **…** actions button, and choose **Undo this change**. Undo puts back only what that change wrote, such as a Page's name or one formula, and keeps every later change. If a later change edited the same thing, nothing is undone, and a notice says what the later change edited: **A later change edited the [field] of [subject], so this change can’t be undone on its own.** Undo the later change first, then this one. In a Scenario, undoing its only edit to something it read from Main returns that item to Main's current version; see [Version history records every change](/concepts/scenarios#version-history-records-every-change). A change a Scenario inherited from Main, whose summary reads **in Main Scenario**, has no **Undo this change** inside the Scenario; undo it in Main. **Undo this change** is shown only when you can edit the Scenario.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The undone entry is struck through and marked **Undone**, and the new entry that records the undo offers **Redo this change**.

## Restore an earlier version

Restoring returns the whole Scenario to an earlier point and rolls back every change made after it. You can ask Ari, naming the Scenario and the point, such as "Restore Main Scenario to how it was yesterday at 5 p.m.", or restore it yourself:

1. In version history, select the entry for the point you want. The Model shows how it looked then, with a banner that reads **Viewing [time] · Read only**.
2. Select **Restore** in the banner, or open the entry's **…** actions button and choose **Restore this version**.
3. Review the dialog, which gives the number of changes it will undo and who made them, and select **Restore version**.

The restore is itself a recorded change, so you can undo it: select **Undo** on the notice that confirms the restore, or undo the restore's entry in version history. Restoring needs full access to the Scenario. On a Snapshot, which is pinned to one moment, **Undo this change** is not shown and **Restore this version** is turned off. In a Scenario other than Main, changes made in Main stay as they are. When Scenarios were branched from this one, the dialog offers to restore them to the same time as well.

To leave the past version without restoring it, select **Back to latest**.
