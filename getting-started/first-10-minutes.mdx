The goal of your first session is a Model of your own business that you have checked once. Ari builds it. You bring what only you know, such as your books, your current spreadsheet, or how the company makes money, and you correct Ari where it guessed. Ari gets closer to your business with your numbers than with estimates.

<Tip>
  Stuck? Ask Ari first. It knows how cfo.ai works and what is in your Model. Try "What can you build for me?", "Walk me through this Page.", or "How can you help me understand where our cash goes?"
</Tip>

## Before you begin

Have one of these ready:

- Your accounting login, such as QuickBooks Online, Xero, or NetSuite. You need admin access, because you approve the connection yourself.
- Your current model, such as an Excel workbook.
- A few facts about the business: what you sell, what you charge, how many customers and employees you have, and how much cash you have.

Set aside 10 to 30 minutes. You can keep working while a large file or a long history loads.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Give Ari your own numbers">
    Choose whichever you have:

    - Connect your data, such as QuickBooks Online, Xero, or NetSuite. You approve access in your browser, and Ari tells you in the chat when the data has loaded. See [Connect and sync your data](/integrations/connect-and-sync-data).
    - Attach your current model, such as an Excel workbook. See [Import an existing spreadsheet model](/getting-started/import-a-spreadsheet-model).
    - Type a few lines about the business. Ari labels every number you did not give it as an assumption.

    If Ari shows a starting card, pick the matching choices. If it posts a brief about your company, correct anything it got wrong.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell Ari what you want to know">
    Name the decision that matters, so Ari builds the right thing. Write it the way you would ask a coworker. For example:

    > Build a model of our business for the next [12 / 18 / 24] months. I want to know [how long our cash lasts / whether we can hire / when we break even].

    For a larger build, Ari may show a plan with its assumptions first. Change any that are wrong, then select **Build it**. Ari builds Pages one at a time, so you can read the first while the next one forms.

    More prompts: [Ari prompts](/ari/prompts).

  </Step>
  <Step title="Check one number you already know">
    Open the first Page and find a number you know, such as last month's revenue or cash in the bank. Right-click the cell and choose **Mention cell in chat**, or select it and press **⌘ L** (Ctrl+L on Windows and Linux). Then ask:

    > Where does this number come from? Show me the source and the formula.

    If it is wrong, tell Ari the right number and why.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Change one assumption">
    Type a new value in the cell yourself, or ask Ari:

    > Move the start date for the next two hires to [month] and show what happens to cash.

    Ari writes the formulas, and the calculation engine does the math, so every connected number updates. To go back, press **⌘ Z** (Ctrl+Z) or use [Version history](/getting-started/build-by-hand#version-history-and-undo). See [Build and edit your Model by hand](/getting-started/build-by-hand) for other changes you can make yourself.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## What done looks like

- At least one Page built from your own data, file, or description.
- One number you checked against a figure you trust.
- One assumption you changed, and the result you saw.

## If something looks wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The numbers do not match my books">
    Ask Ari to compare the Model with the source for one closed month and list every account that differs. Often the connection is still loading, or the Model's **Last close**, the last month of Actuals, is earlier than you expect.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Ari built something different from what I wanted">
    Tell Ari what to change, and it updates the Model. You can also undo anything you try: every change, yours or Ari's, is saved in **Version history**, the clock icon in the Page header. Undo one change or restore an earlier version there, press **⌘ Z** (Ctrl+Z), or ask Ari "Undo that last change." See [Version history and undo](/getting-started/build-by-hand#version-history-and-undo).

    To redirect a long build, select **Stop** and say what you want instead. See [Steer, stop, and approve during a chat](/ari/during-a-conversation).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The reply stopped partway">
    Select **Retry** on **Ari stopped before finishing**. Anything Ari saved before it stopped is still in the Model.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Getting help

- Ask Ari. Try:
  - "What can you build for me?"
  - "Walk me through this Page."
  - "How can you help me understand where our cash goes?"
  - "Why did expenses go up last month?"
  - "What should I build next?"
  - "How do I connect payroll?"
