To add a user to your cfo.ai workspace, invite them by email, from Settings or by asking Ari.

Choose a role for each person. An administrator can see and edit every Page, invite others, and manage billing and settings. A viewer can read every Page and table and cannot change anything or start a chat with Ari; see [Administrators and viewers](/concepts/sharing-and-access#administrators-and-viewers). To let someone outside the company read reports without joining, send a share link instead; see [Give someone outside the workspace read-only access](#give-someone-outside-the-workspace-read-only-access).

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Ask Ari">
    Ask Ari to invite one or more people:

    > Invite dana@example.com and lee@example.com to the workspace.

    To invite someone who should read and not edit, name the viewer role:

    > Invite sam@example.com as a viewer.

    One request invites people in one role, so ask for administrators and viewers in separate requests.

    You can also select **Ask Ari** beside **Admins and viewers** in **Settings → Workspace**; see [Admins and viewers](/reference/settings#admins-and-viewers) for what that chat starts with.

    Ari shows exactly who it will invite and waits for you to confirm. You can add a welcome for the people you invite, with any of these:

    - A short note.
    - A Page to open first.
    - Up to three questions they can send to Ari with one click.
    - A saved Guide, offered as a tour.

    For example:

    > Invite dana@example.com with a note saying she'll own the hiring plan. Open the Headcount Page first and offer the onboarding tour.

    If someone already belongs to the workspace without the administrator role, such as a viewer, ask Ari to make them an administrator, or enter their address under **Invite people** in Settings with **Role** set to **Admin**. Either way changes their role and sends no invitation.
  </Tab>

      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose the role">

      </Step>

      <Step title="Send the invitation">
        Select **Add admin** or **Add viewer**; the button follows the role. The form reads **Admin invitation created for** or **Viewer invitation created for** the address, and the person appears in the **Admins and viewers** list marked **Invited** or **Invited as viewer**. If you chose **Admin** and the address already belongs to the workspace without the administrator role, the person becomes an administrator at once, no email is sent, and the form reads that the address **is now an admin.**
      </Step>

Adding people does not change your price: a plan is priced for the workspace, not per person. See [Plans, AI usage, and connection slots](/concepts/plans-and-usage).

## What the invited person sees

The person gets an email invitation to the workspace. From a workspace with a brand, the email comes from your brand's name **via cfo.ai**, shows the brand, and ends with **Powered by cfo.ai**; see [Brand your workspace](/guides/brand-your-workspace). When they sign in with the invited address, the workspace is in their account. If you added a welcome, the email carries your note when you wrote one, and its link opens the Page you chose when you chose one. The whole welcome shows in Ari's empty chat when they first open the workspace. It is labeled **Note from** your name, with **Take the tour** for the Guide and **Open** followed by the Page's name. The person can dismiss it.

The email is the same for both roles and does not name the role. A viewer cannot chat with Ari, so the welcome in Ari's empty chat, with its questions and tour, does not reach a viewer. A viewer's email still carries your note, and its link opens the Page you chose.

## Resend an invitation or fix one that did not arrive

An invitation stays pending, marked **Invited** or **Invited as viewer**, until the person joins. If they cannot find the email:

1. Check the address in the **Admins and viewers** list in **Settings → Workspace**. If it is wrong, cancel that invitation and invite the right address.
2. Ask them to check their spam or junk folder.
3. Send the email again: enter the same address under **Invite people** with the same **Role** and select **Add admin** or **Add viewer**, or ask Ari to resend the invitation. Resending keeps any welcome you added.
4. Or skip the email: ask them to go to [app.cfo.ai](https://app.cfo.ai) and sign in with exactly the invited address. A pending invitation is accepted when that address signs in, and the workspace appears.

You can invite a teammate at a personal address, such as a Gmail address, even when they have no cfo.ai account yet. They must sign in with exactly the address you invited. If they see **Use your work email**, they signed in with a personal address that has no pending invitation: check that the invitation is still marked **Invited** or **Invited as viewer** and that they used that address. An account with a personal address can join workspaces it is invited to, and cannot create a workspace of its own; see [Work email required for new accounts](/reference/interface#work-email-required-for-new-accounts).

If the person signs in with a different address, such as the one on their Slack profile, invite that address instead.

## Cancel an invitation

In **Settings → Workspace**, open the menu on the person's **Invited** or **Invited as viewer** row, choose **Cancel invitation**, and confirm. You can also ask Ari, for example "Cancel the invitation for dana@example.com." Ari names the invitation it will cancel and waits for you to confirm. Once it is canceled, they can no longer join through it. If they joined before you confirmed, they keep their access; to take it away, remove them.

## Change a viewer into an administrator

In **Settings → Workspace**, enter the viewer's address under **Invite people** with **Role** set to **Admin** and select **Add admin**, or ask Ari to make them an administrator. The change takes effect at once and sends no email.

You cannot change an administrator into a viewer. Remove them, then invite them again as a viewer.

## Remove a teammate

In **Settings → Workspace**, open the menu on the person's **Admin** or **Viewer** row, choose **Remove member**, and confirm. You can also ask Ari, for example "Remove lee@example.com from the workspace."

The person loses access to this workspace right away, and any pending invitations for their email are canceled. Their access to other workspaces and their cfo.ai account stay as they are. To delete an account, see [Cancel your subscription or delete your data](/guides/cancel-or-delete).

You cannot remove yourself or the workspace's last administrator, in Settings or through Ari. To leave a workspace, ask another administrator to remove you.

In a workspace billed in the app, if the person you remove receives the workspace's invoices and receipts, those emails go to a remaining administrator afterward; the removal dialog says so. To send them somewhere else, change the recipient first in **Settings → Billing**; see [Billing recipient](/reference/plans-and-billing#billing-recipient).

## See who was given or lost access

To check who joined the workspace, who was removed, and who made each change over a period, ask Ari:

> Who was given or lost access to this workspace in November, and who made each change?

Ari lists each recorded change from the oldest: when it happened, who made it, who gained or lost access, and the access before and after. Only an administrator can ask. Ari can go back only to the time cfo.ai began recording access changes, and it tells you when the period you asked about starts earlier. For what the record holds and what it leaves out, see [Access history](/reference/settings#access-history).

## If an invitation is refused

- **workspace member already exists** when you add a viewer means the address already belongs to the workspace, for example because the person signed in to keep a Page you shared with a link. Remove them, then invite them as a viewer.
- A message about a pending admin or viewer invitation means the address already holds an invitation in the other role. Cancel that invitation, then invite again.

Every message is listed in [Invite people](/reference/settings#invite-people).

## Give someone outside the workspace read-only access

For a teammate who should read and not edit, invite them as a viewer. To show reports to an investor, a board member, or a client without giving them access to everything, share a Page with a link instead of inviting them. A link opens the Pages you choose, read-only, without a cfo.ai account. See [Share a Page outside your workspace](/guides/share-a-page) and [Sharing and access](/concepts/sharing-and-access).

To let one named person open one Page after they sign in, ask Ari to invite them to that Page; see [Invite one person by email to view a Page](/guides/share-a-page#invite-one-person-by-email-to-view-a-page).
