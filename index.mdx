cfo.ai gives you Ari, an AI finance coworker. Ask it whether you can afford to hire, why spending went up, or how long your cash lasts. Ari answers by building and updating a financial Model of your business, and you can open that Model, check every number, and change the assumptions yourself.

> Can we hire three engineers in September? Show the effect on payroll, cash, and cash runway.

## Connect an AI agent

AI clients such as Claude Code and Codex can work on your Model through the cfo.ai MCP server. To connect one, see [Connect Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, or another AI tool to cfo.ai](/guides/connect-an-ai-client). If you are building an agent, start with [cfo.ai for AI agents](/agents).

## Get help

- Ask Ari in the app. It knows how cfo.ai works and what is in your Model: "How do I connect payroll?" or "Walk me through this Page."
- Press **⌘ ?** (**Ctrl ?** on Windows and Linux) in the app to open the Help center.
- Email the team at [success@cfo.ai](mailto:success@cfo.ai).
