Your first Model is a starting point. Over the next five days, take one short task a day. Each day builds on the one before: a forecast you understand, a Page people can read, a decision you can test, more of your real data, and a Model that keeps working when you are not in it.

Each task has a prompt you can paste into Ari, and [Ari prompts](/ari/prompts) has more. Replace the words in brackets with your own. Start a new chat for each day with **⌘ /** (Ctrl+/ on Windows and Linux) so each conversation stays about one task.

If you have not built a first Model yet, start with [Your first 10 minutes](/getting-started/first-10-minutes). If its numbers did not match your books, fix that first; see [If something looks wrong](/getting-started/first-10-minutes#if-something-looks-wrong).

<Steps>
  <Step icon="chart-line" title="Day 2: Grow your Model into a forecast">
    **Why:** a forecast is where the Model starts answering questions about the future, and every number in it should be one you can explain.

    > Extend this into a [12]-month forecast. Drive revenue from [new customers, pricing, and churn] and costs from [headcount and our biggest vendors]. Put the assumptions where I can see and change them.

    Ari writes the formulas; the calculation engine does the math. Then pick one forecast number and trace it: right-click the cell, choose **Mention cell in chat**, and ask:

    > How is this calculated? Which assumptions move it the most?

    For a whole table, hover its left edge, click the six-dot handle, and choose **Explain with Ari**. Every change Ari made is listed in [Version history](/getting-started/build-by-hand#version-history-and-undo), the clock icon in the Page header.

    **Done when:** you can name the two or three assumptions that move your forecast the most.

    **Next:** your forecast is right. Tomorrow, make it something other people can read.

  </Step>
  <Step icon="palette" title="Day 3: Build a Page you would share">
    **Why:** a Page is how the Model reaches the people who use it, such as your board, investors, or team. Canvas blocks make it something they want to open.

    A Canvas block is a custom visual Ari writes for you, so it is not limited to standard chart types. Describe what you want to see, and Ari builds it from your live Model:

    > Build a [board / monthly] summary Page with a row of our five key numbers and their change since last month, a cash runway gauge, and a slider so I can try different hiring dates and see the effect on cash.

    For ideas, select **Browse Canvas block library** in the chat composer and pick a template to start from. Each template is a recipe Ari adapts to your Model.

    <Frame caption="The Canvas block library button sits at the bottom left of the chat composer.">
      <img src="/images/canvas-block-library-button.png" width="367" alt="The Ari chat composer with the palette icon at the bottom left highlighted and labeled Browse Canvas block library." />
    </Frame>

    More prompts to try are in [Ari prompts](/ari/prompts#interactive-visuals) and [Charts and custom visualizations](/dashboards/charts-and-custom-visualizations).

    When it looks right, select **Share** in the Page header to create a read-only link. Anyone with the link sees the live Page without signing in. See [Share a Page and choose its Scenario](/dashboards/share-pages-and-scenarios).

    **Done when:** you have sent the link to one person.

    **Next:** your Page shows one plan. Tomorrow, test another.

  </Step>
  <Step icon="code-branch" title="Day 4: Test a decision in a Scenario">
    **Why:** a Scenario lets you try a plan without changing your main Model, so you can see what a decision costs before you make it.

    > Create a Scenario called [Hire two engineers in Q1]. [Add two engineers at $X per year starting in January.] Compare cash and cash runway with Main, and add the comparison to my [board] Page.

    **Done when:** you can say what the decision does to cash runway, and your Page shows it next to Main.

    See [Create and compare Scenarios](/scenarios/create-and-compare-scenarios).

    **Next:** some of your forecast still rests on estimates. Tomorrow, replace one with real data.

  </Step>
  <Step icon="link" title="Day 5: Replace an estimate with real data">
    **Why:** every source you connect turns a guess into a number you can trust, and every later answer improves with it.

    Pick the estimate you trust least, such as headcount, pipeline, or a big vendor, and connect the system that holds it:

    > Connect [Gusto / Rippling / HubSpot] and replace the [headcount / pipeline] assumptions with real data. Tell me what changed in the forecast.

    See [Browse the integrations directory](/integrations/integrations-directory) and, for payroll, [Build a Headcount page](/headcount/build-a-headcount-page).

    **Done when:** one assumption in your forecast now comes from a connected source.

    **Next:** your Model now updates from your systems every day. Tomorrow, have Ari tell you what changed.

  </Step>
  <Step icon="clock" title="Day 6: Let Ari keep watch">
    **Why:** the Model keeps working when you are not in it, and you hear about what matters without opening the app.

    Start with something you will get this week:

    > Every Monday at 8am [your time zone], summarize last week's cash in and out, show our current cash runway, and flag any expense that looks different from our recent pattern. Include a link to my [board] Page.

    Other weekly ideas:

    - "Every Friday at 4pm, tell me how this month is tracking against the forecast so far and what to watch next week."
    - "Every Monday, list any new hires, departures, or salary changes from payroll and show the effect on this year's payroll cost."

    Once your books close each month, a monthly check works well too:

    - "On the 5th of every month, compare last month's actuals with the forecast and explain the three biggest differences."

    Ari asks where to deliver the result, your Slack direct messages or your email, before it saves the Trigger. See [Schedule recurring work with Triggers](/automation/triggers).

    If you have a process you repeat yourself, such as how you close the month, you can teach it to Ari once as a [Skill](/automation/skills).

    **Done when:** you have one Trigger saved, and its first result is on the way.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## After the first week

Connected sources refresh every day on their own. Each month, after your books close:

1. Read the comparison of actuals and forecast, from your Trigger or by asking Ari.
2. Update the assumptions that turned out wrong.
3. Open your shared Page and check it before it goes out.

After your first month, write down what you still do by hand, such as copying numbers into a deck or rebuilding the same table. Ask Ari to take each one over:

> Here is what I still do by hand each month: [list]. Which of these can you do for me as a Skill or a Trigger? Set up the first one.

## Get better answers from Ari

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Talk to Ari plainly">
    Instead of: a long, detailed prompt that spells out every table and formula.

    Try: "Build our P&L for next year from QuickBooks." Ari works out the details and asks when it needs something from you.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Say the decision, not the report">
    Instead of: "Build a forecast."

    Try: "Build a 12-month forecast so I can see whether we can hire two engineers in Q1."
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Point at what you mean">
    Instead of: "Why is the revenue number in that table wrong?"

    Try: right-click the cell, choose **Mention cell in chat**, and ask "Why is this lower than last month?"
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Say whether you want an answer or a change">
    Instead of: "What about gross margin?"

    Try: "Explain why gross margin fell in March. Do not change the Model." Or: "Update the gross margin formula in the Downside Scenario."
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Correct Ari when it guesses">
    Instead of: starting over in a new chat.

    Try: "Our customers pay annually up front, not monthly. Fix the revenue timing and remember this." See [What Ari remembers](/ari/what-ari-remembers).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Steer a long build">
    Instead of: waiting for a build that is going the wrong way.

    Try: select **Stop**, or type a correction while Ari works; Ari reads it as soon as it finishes what it is doing. See [Steer, stop, and approve during a chat](/ari/during-a-conversation).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Undo instead of starting over">
    Instead of: deleting a Page or opening a new workspace when a change goes wrong.

    Try: open [Version history](/getting-started/build-by-hand#version-history-and-undo) and select **Undo this change** or **Restore this version**, or ask Ari "Undo that last change."
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

For more, see [Ask Ari better questions](/ari/ask-better-questions).
