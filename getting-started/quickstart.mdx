cfo.ai gives you one workspace for financial data, planning, analysis, and reporting. Ari helps you build a useful Model from connected sources, an uploaded file, or clearly stated assumptions.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your workspace">
    Sign in at [app.cfo.ai](https://app.cfo.ai). Your workspace holds your Model, Pages, Scenarios, data connections, and team settings.

    During onboarding, Ari may share what it knows about your company and ask you to confirm or correct it. That context helps Ari choose a sensible starting point.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to begin">
    You can connect existing business data or start a Model from scratch.

    - **Connect your data** when you want accounting, HR, CRM, spreadsheet, or database history to inform the first Model.
    - **Start from scratch** when you want to work from a business description and explicit assumptions first.
    - **Import an existing model** when a workbook or report already contains the financial structure you want to rebuild.

    Connecting data is optional. You can start with assumptions and connect the supporting systems later.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell Ari what you want to build">
    Start with the question or decision that matters most.

    > Build a monthly financial model for my software company, including revenue, headcount, operating expenses, and cash.

    Ari uses available company context and source data, identifies any assumptions, and creates the Pages, tables, and calculations needed for the request.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the first result">
    Open the Page Ari creates and check the key assumptions, date range, Variables, and business categories. Ask Ari to explain a number, adjust an assumption, or add detail where it matters.

    > Show me the assumptions behind revenue growth, and break the forecast down by customer segment.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide what comes next">
    Add another source, create a Scenario, prepare a report, or save a recurring workflow. Your Model grows from the same underlying data and calculations instead of becoming a set of disconnected spreadsheets.
  </Step>
</Steps>
