Your workspace is your company's home in cfo.ai. Everything you build or connect belongs to that workspace, including your Model, reporting Pages, Scenarios, connections, team, and saved workflows.

## One Model, many useful views

A Model represents how your business operates across time. Its Variables hold the values you care about, its Dimensions explain how those values are organized, and its formulas connect assumptions, source data, and financial results.

A Page presents a useful part of that Model. One Page might show a profit and loss statement, another might show headcount, and a third might present a board report. These Pages can reuse Variables such as Revenue and Cash and organize those values by Dimensions such as Department without creating separate copies.

You, Ari, and any authorized external agent work on that same Model. You can open what Ari creates, inspect its assumptions, and change it directly instead of treating a conversation as the only place the answer exists.

## The main parts of your workspace

- **Pages** organize tables, charts, and written context in the sidebar.
- **Table Blocks** show live Model values across time and business categories.
- **Scenarios** let you compare an alternative plan with Main.
- **Connections** hold the systems that supply your financial and operating data, the MCP servers Ari can call, and the APIs it can reach. Open them from the sidebar.
- **Ari** is your AI finance coworker. It answers questions, explains results, and builds or changes the Model when you ask.
- **Settings** hold workspace details, connections, Skills, billing, usage, and personal preferences.

## What is in Settings

Open Settings from the sidebar. Its sections are:

- **General** — the workspace name, the **Date & Time** defaults (default date granularity, default date range, and Last close), and appearance.
- **Profile** — your own account details.
- **Ari** — the sound Ari plays when it finishes a reply, and the **Slack** connection that lets you talk to Ari from Slack. See [Personal preferences](/workspace-settings/personal-theme).
- **Connections** — the MCP servers and APIs Ari can call. See [Browse the integrations directory](/integrations/integrations-directory).
- **Skills** — the workspace's saved Skills. See [Save a workflow as a Skill](/automation/skills).
- **Billing** and **Usage** — your plan, AI usage, credits, spend limit, and auto-reload. See [Billing and plan](/workspace-settings/billing).

There is no team screen; ask Ari to invite or remove administrators. See [Workspace and team settings](/workspace-settings/team-and-workspace).

## Search your workspace

The **Search** field at the top of the sidebar, above **Chats**, opens a search window. Press **⌘ K** (Ctrl+K on Windows and Linux) to open or close it from anywhere; on a phone, the field sits at the top of the navigation drawer. The window's placeholder reads **Search pages, chats, and more**. Before you type, it lists the Pages you opened recently in this browser under **Today**, **Yesterday**, **Past week**, and **Past 30 days**.

Results are grouped as **Pages**, **Blocks**, **Variables**, **Dimensions**, **Dimension items**, **Workspaces**, and **Chats**, up to eight per group:

- **Pages** match by title, and from two characters also by the words in their text blocks. **Blocks** are named tables and titled charts and Canvas blocks.
- **Variables** and **Dimensions** are listed when a table on a Page you can read shows them. **Dimension items** are listed when a table shows their Dimension on its rows, and each row names its Dimension.
- **Workspaces** are your other workspaces, by name. **Chats** are your titled chats; when none of the loaded chats match, cfo.ai also searches your older chats by title.

Every word you type must match. Matching ignores case and accents, treats punctuation as a space, and matches the start of a word, so `rev` finds Revenue; from five letters, a small typo is forgiven. The older-chat search is stricter: every word must appear in the title as typed.

Use **↑** and **↓** to move, **Enter** to open, **⌘ Enter** (or **⌘ click**) to open in a new tab, and **Esc** to close. Opening a Page shows it in the current Scenario; a block scrolls into view and flashes; a Variable opens its table and marks its row; a workspace switches with a full page load. You see only the Pages, chats, and model objects you can read. Search is not available while you are viewing a past version.

Press **⌘ /** (Ctrl+/) to start a new chat with Ari from anywhere, and **⌘ L** (Ctrl+L) to mention the selected cell or block in the current chat.

## Use cfo.ai on a phone

A phone browser opens the same workspace at native scale, one surface at a time. The header's **Open navigation** button opens a drawer with the **Search** field, your chats, the Model section with its Pages, and Connections; picking a chat, a search result, or **New chat** closes it. A chat header offers **Open page panel** to read a Page beside the conversation, and a Page header offers **New chat**; **Back to chat** returns from a Page to the conversation.

Pages are read-only in this presentation, whatever your access: you can read tables and Canvas blocks, and a table's **Table block settings** menu offers only **Show formula bar** and **Show formula columns** beside **Mention table in chat**, but you cannot edit cells, add rows or blocks, or create Pages or Scenarios. An empty state says **Pages are read-only in this browser. Use Desktop Site to create a page.** Ask your browser for **Desktop Site** to get the desktop product, editing included, on the same device. A tablet gets the same read-only presentation unless it asks for the desktop site.

## Work from the current Page

When you ask Ari about the Page you have open, Ari can use that Page as context. Point to a table, row, or cell when you want an explanation tied to a particular number.

> Why is payroll higher in April on this Page?

> Add a chart showing the last 12 months of revenue above this table.

> Create a Scenario that assumes we hire two account executives in June.

## Switch context when it matters

If you have access to more than one workspace, use the workspace switcher to choose the right company or entity. Within a workspace, name the Scenario when a question or change should apply somewhere other than the one you are viewing.
