Your first session has one goal: a Model of your own business that you have checked at least once. Ari does the building. You bring the facts only you have, such as your books, your current spreadsheet, or how the company makes money, and you correct Ari where it guessed.

That second part matters. Ari builds a closer fit when it starts from your numbers instead of estimates, and every correction you make in the first session carries into later answers.

<Tip>
  **Stuck? Ask Ari first.** Ari knows how cfo.ai works and what is in your Model. Try "What can you build for me?", "Walk me through this Page.", or "How can you help me understand where our cash goes?"
</Tip>

## Before you begin

Have one of these ready:

- **Your accounting login**, such as QuickBooks Online, Xero, or NetSuite. You need to be an administrator of the company file, because you approve the connection yourself.
- **Your current model**, such as an Excel workbook.
- **A few facts about the business**, if you have neither: what you sell, what you charge, how many customers and employees you have, and how much cash is in the bank.

Set aside 10 to 30 minutes, depending on how much you bring. You can keep working while a large file or a long history finishes loading.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Give Ari your own numbers">
    However your workspace starts, whether with a starting card, an empty chat, or a Model Ari already began, your first job is the same: give Ari something real about your business. You get the closest fit when Ari works from your own numbers. Choose whichever you have:

    - **Connect your data**, such as QuickBooks Online, Xero, or NetSuite. You approve access in your browser, and Ari tells you in the chat when the data has loaded. See [Connect and sync your data](/integrations/connect-and-sync-data).
    - **Attach your current model**, such as an Excel workbook. See [Import an existing spreadsheet model](/getting-started/import-a-spreadsheet-model).
    - **Type a few lines** about the business if you have neither: what you sell, what you charge, and how much cash is in the bank. Ari labels every number you did not give it as an assumption.

    If Ari shows a starting card, pick the choices that match. If it posts a brief about your company, correct anything it got wrong, such as what you sell or how you charge.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell Ari what you want to know">
    Tell Ari the decision that matters, so it builds the right thing. If Ari has already started a Model, this steers it; if not, this starts one. Write it the way you would ask a coworker; you do not need a perfect prompt. For example:

    > Build a model of our business for the next [12 / 18 / 24] months. I want to know [how long our cash lasts / whether we can hire / when we break even].

    For a larger build, Ari may show a plan with the assumptions it intends to use. Change any that are wrong, then select **Build it**. Ari builds Pages one at a time, so you can read the first Page while the next one forms.

    For more prompts to start from, see [Ari prompts](/ari/prompts).

  </Step>
  <Step title="Check one number you already know">
    Open the first Page Ari built. Find a number you know by heart, such as last month's revenue, payroll, or cash in the bank.

    Right-click that cell and choose **Mention cell in chat**, or select it and press **⌘ L** (Ctrl+L on Windows and Linux). Then ask:

    > Where does this number come from? Show me the source and the formula.

    If it is wrong, tell Ari what the right number is and why. That correction is worth more than any prompt.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Change one assumption">
    Pick an assumption you care about. Type a new value in the cell yourself, or ask Ari:

    > Move the start date for the next two hires to [month] and show what happens to cash.

    The calculation engine recalculates every connected number. Ari writes formulas; it does not do the math in the chat. If you do not like the result, press **⌘ Z** (Ctrl+Z) or undo it from [Version history](/getting-started/build-by-hand#version-history-and-undo).

    You can make any change by hand like this. See [Build and edit your Model by hand](/getting-started/build-by-hand).

  </Step>
</Steps>

## What done looks like

After your first session, you should have:

- At least one Page built from your own data, file, or description.
- One number you checked against a figure you trust.
- One assumption you changed, and the result you saw.

## If something looks wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The numbers do not match my books">
    Ask Ari to compare the Model with the source for one closed month and list every account that differs. A common cause is that the connection is still loading, or that the Model's last closed month is earlier than you expect.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Ari built something different from what I wanted">
    That is part of the process. Working with Ari is back and forth: tell it what to change, and it updates the Model.

    Nothing you try is permanent. Every change, whether you made it or Ari did, is saved in **Version history**, the clock icon in the Page header. Undo one change or restore an earlier version there, press **⌘ Z** (Ctrl+Z), or ask Ari "Undo that last change." See [Version history and undo](/getting-started/build-by-hand#version-history-and-undo).

    If a long build is heading the wrong way, select **Stop** and say what you want instead. See [Steer, stop, and approve during a chat](/ari/during-a-conversation).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The reply stopped partway">
    Select **Retry** on **Ari stopped before finishing**. Anything Ari saved before the stop is still in the Model.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Getting help

- **Ask Ari first.** Ari knows how cfo.ai works and what is in your Model. Try:
  - "What can you build for me?"
  - "Walk me through this Page."
  - "How can you help me understand where our cash goes?"
  - "Why did expenses go up last month?"
  - "What should I build next?"
  - "How do I connect payroll?"
- **In the docs:** start with [Meet Ari](/ari/meet-ari), [Ari prompts](/ari/prompts), and [Ask Ari better questions](/ari/ask-better-questions).
- **From the team:** email [success@cfo.ai](mailto:success@cfo.ai).
