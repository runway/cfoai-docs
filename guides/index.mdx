Each guide walks you through one task, such as connecting QuickBooks, fixing a connection, or sharing a Page. The guides assume you know your way around. If you are new, start with [Build your first Model](/tutorials/first-model). Most tasks can be done by asking Ari or by hand, and the guides show both.

- [Connect payroll, HR, CRM, payments, or a database](/guides/connect-other-sources)
- [Connect a Google Sheet or Drive file](/guides/connect-a-google-sheet)
- [Upload a CSV as a data source](/guides/upload-a-csv)
- [Import an existing spreadsheet model](/guides/import-a-spreadsheet-model)
