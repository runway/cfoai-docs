cfo.ai has three plans: Starter, Growth, and Enterprise. A plan is bought for a workspace, not per person, and its price is shown in the app. Billing lives in two Settings sections, **Billing** and **Usage**, which every member can open because every member is an administrator. A workspace billed outside the app shows neither. Ari can read your plan, usage, balance, spend limit, and connection slots, but it cannot change billing; it tells you which Settings section to use. For how plans work, see [Plans, AI usage, and connection slots](/concepts/plans-and-usage).

## Plans

The plan cards show each plan's current price. Starter and Growth come in monthly and annual versions, and include no premium integrations. For which integrations are standard and which are premium, see the [integrations directory](/reference/integrations#standard-and-premium-integrations). The cfo.ai MCP server works in any workspace that has started its trial or a plan, whichever plan it is; see [Connect Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, or another AI tool](/guides/connect-an-ai-client).

### Starter

The card reads **Build and run the finance model.** It lists:

- Standard AI usage that resets monthly
- Up to 5 standard integrations
- Up to 500K synced rows per month
- The MCP server and Slack app
- Proactive alerts and Triggers
- Unlimited Models and Scenarios
- 1 dedicated workspace

### Growth

The card reads **Scale the workflows that are already running in Starter.** It lists:

- Everything in Starter
- A multiple of Starter's AI usage, shown on the card
- Up to 8 standard integrations
- Up to 2M synced rows per month

### Enterprise

The card reads **Connect a complex stack and add hands-on finance support.** It lists:

- A custom price and AI credits
- Premium integrations, including NetSuite and data warehouses
- Multiple workspaces
- SSO / SAML and priority support
- A dedicated Forward Deployed Finance Partner and a shared Slack channel

Enterprise is not chosen from its card: **Talk to sales** opens a scheduler to book a call. If the scheduler cannot open, a notice reads **Couldn’t open scheduling**.

## Monthly and annual billing

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| The switch | **Monthly** and **Annual** above the plan cards, shown when annual plans are offered. **Annual** names its saving as **Annual — save** followed by a percentage, or **Annual — save up to** a percentage when plans save different amounts. |
| Which side it opens on | **Annual** when you choose your first plan. In **Settings → Billing**, the cadence of the plan you have. |
| Annual prices | Shown per month, with **billed annually** and the yearly amount. An annual plan is charged for the year at the start of each term. |
| Changing cadence | Moving between monthly and annual on the same plan is scheduled for your next renewal. Moving to a higher plan and changing cadence in one step is not offered. |

## Free trial

A free trial lasts 14 days and needs a card to start. When it ends, the paid plan starts and charges the card on file, unless you cancel before the end date.

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Length | 14 days |
| How many | One per workspace. A workspace that had a trial, including a canceled one, pays from the first day of its next plan. |
| Card | Required to start. Nothing is charged when the trial starts. |
| When it ends | The paid plan starts on its own and charges the card on file. |
| Canceling | Cancel any time before the end and nothing is charged; the trial still runs to its end date. |
| Changing plans | A plan change during a trial applies right away at no charge, and the trial keeps its end date. |
| AI usage | The trial includes its own AI usage. Once it is used up, **Start paid plan** lets you end the trial early, pay, and reset your included usage. Before then, the plan cannot be started early. |

| Message | Where |
| --- | --- |
| **Try cfo.ai free for 14 days** / **\$0 today. Cancel anytime before it ends and you won’t be charged, and you still get all 14 days free.** | Plan page, first plan |
| **Choose a plan to continue** / **Your free trial has already been used. Paid plans charge immediately.** | Plan page, when the workspace had a trial |
| **We ask for a card to confirm you’re a real person and keep** [plan] **active after the trial. Cancel in Settings before** [date] **and you won’t be charged. You still get all 14 days free.** | **Add a payment method**, starting a trial |
| **This plan has no free trial. Starting it charges your card immediately.** | **Add a payment method**, plan with no trial |
| **By starting a plan, you agree to the Standard Terms and Conditions and acknowledge the Privacy Policy.** | Plan page; see [Privacy, security, and legal documents](/reference/privacy-and-legal) |

## Plan chooser

**Change** next to your plan in **Settings → Billing** opens the plan chooser, headed **Choose your plan**, or **Change your trial** during a trial. With no plan, the button reads **Choose a plan**.

| Button on a plan card | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Start free trial** | Starts a 14-day trial of the plan. |
| **Subscribe to** [plan] / **Choose** [plan] | Starts the plan and charges it today. |
| **Switch to** [plan] | Changes your plan. A confirmation shows the cost first. |
| **Your current plan** / **Your current trial** | The plan you have, marked **Current plan** or **Current trial**. |
| **Keep** [plan] | Calls off a scheduled change or a cancellation. |
| **Keep and switch to** [plan] | Calls off a cancellation and switches plan. |
| **Starts** [date] / **Change scheduled** | The plan you are switching to at renewal. |
| **Contact support to change plan** | Your plan is a custom plan that cannot be changed here. |
| **Talk to sales** | Enterprise. |

A card you cannot choose is grayed out, and pointing at its button says why:

| Reason | Text |
| --- | --- |
| More integrations connected than the plan allows | [plan] **includes** [number] **standard integrations; you have** [number] **connected. Disconnect integrations to switch.** For a class the plan lacks: [plan] **doesn't include premium integrations; you have** [number] **connected.** |
| A higher plan on the other cadence, from annual | **Your annual plan runs until** [date]**. Switch to** [plan] **on annual billing to upgrade now, or move to monthly billing when your plan renews.** |
| A higher plan on the other cadence, from monthly | **Switch to** [plan] **on monthly billing first. You can move to annual billing when your plan renews on** [date]**.** |
| Not offered for this workspace | [plan] **isn't available for this workspace — contact support.** |

The card dialogs do not close when you click outside them, so a card you typed is not lost. Close them with **Cancel** or **Esc**.

### Billing history

Each row shows the date, **Credit top-up** for a usage purchase, the amount, and a status: **Paid**, **Failed**, **Processing**, **Due**, **Pending**, **Refunded**, or **Void**. **View** opens the invoice or receipt in a new tab. **View all** opens the full list when there are more rows. With no rows, it reads **Nothing here yet. Invoices and credit purchases will appear here.**

## When the workspace locks

A workspace locks when it has no plan, such as after a canceled plan ends, or when a renewal payment still fails after retries. While it is locked, nobody can open its Pages or chat with Ari, and its connected sources stop refreshing.

| Screen | Who sees it | What it says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plan chooser | Administrators, when there is no plan | The plan cards, with a heading such as **Choose a plan to continue**. Choosing a plan unlocks the workspace. |
| **We couldn’t charge your card** | Administrators, when payment failed | **Your subscription payment didn’t go through. Update your card and we’ll try again straight away. Access comes back as soon as it succeeds.**, your payment methods, and **Pay now**. |
| **This workspace is unavailable** | Members who cannot change billing | **A workspace admin needs to update billing before anyone can get back in.** |

Each screen offers **Log out** and the help@cfo.ai address. When the workspace has connected sources, the screen says when they last refreshed: **Your connected source was last refreshed on** [date]**. Your connected source is not being refreshed while this workspace has no plan.**, or **while this workspace’s billing is locked.** With several sources, it gives the newest refresh of any of them.

## Notices in the chat

When billing stops Ari, its reply ends with a message that starts **I had to stop here**; every one is listed in [Chat controls and messages](/reference/chat#billing-stops-out-of-ai-usage-or-over-a-limit). The **AI usage recovery** panel above the message box then names the problem and offers a fix:

| Panel message | Button |
| --- | --- |
| **This workspace is out of AI usage.** | **Usage settings**, and a purchase button such as **Add \$100** |
| **This workspace reached its monthly AI spend limit.** | **Adjust limit** |
| **Automatic top-up failed — the card on file was declined.** | **Update payment method** |
| **More AI usage is still being added to this workspace.** | **Usage settings** |
| **This workspace's plan isn't active.** | **Review billing** |
| **The workspace's subscription payment is failing.** | **Update payment method** |
| **This workspace hasn't started its trial yet.** | **Open billing** |
| **This workspace reached a limit of its current plan.** | **Open billing** |
| **This wouldn't fit the limits of the plan this workspace is switching to.** | **Open billing** |
| **This workspace's billing status couldn't be checked.** | **Open billing** |

After a purchase, the panel reads **Usage added. This workspace will update in a moment.** and may suggest **Turn on auto-reload to avoid running out of AI usage next time.** with **Enable auto-reload**. In a trial whose usage is used up, it offers **Start plan & save**. Teammates who cannot buy usage see **Ask a workspace admin to add usage.**

While auto-reload buys usage, the chat shows **Adding AI usage to your workspace…**, then **AI usage added to your workspace.**

Before usage runs out, one notice at a time can appear above the message box:

| Notice | When | Button |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [percent] **of your workspace's monthly AI usage remains.** | Included usage falls to 50%, 25%, 10%, and 5% left. Below 1%, it reads **Less than 1%**. | **View usage** |
| **Your workspace reached its monthly top-up limit.** | Purchases this cycle reached the spend limit. | **Adjust limit** |
| **AI usage is low, but the monthly top-up limit keeps auto-reload from adding more.** | Auto-reload is blocked by the spend limit. | **Adjust limit** |

Dismissing a notice hides it until the next threshold or the next billing cycle.

## Rules and limits

- Every member is an administrator, so every member can change the plan, buy usage, and change the spend limit and auto-reload. Adding people does not change the price.
- Canceling keeps the plan until the end of the term you paid for, and a trial until its end date. Under the Standard Terms and Conditions, fees already paid are not refunded.
- You can undo a cancellation any time before it takes effect.
- A subscription cannot be paused.
- A workspace cannot be deleted from Settings. See [Cancel your subscription or delete your data](/guides/cancel-or-delete).
- Adding an integration needs a started trial or an active plan with a free slot. For the messages, see [Connection statuses and actions](/reference/connection-statuses).
- For the trial length and purchase limits with every other limit, see [Limits and file types](/reference/limits).
