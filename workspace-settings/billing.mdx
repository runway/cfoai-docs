Ari can read your workspace's billing status directly, so you don't need to leave the conversation to check your plan or usage. Ari can't change billing itself — every answer points you to the specific Settings section to make the actual change.

## What Ari can tell you

Ask Ari about:

- **Plan** — your current plan, price, status, renewal date, and any scheduled change.
- **Usage** — your monthly AI usage as a percentage of the plan's allotment, your extra-usage balance, whether auto-reload is on, and your spending limit.
- **Integrations** — how many standard and premium connections your plan allows, and how many you're using. See [Integrations Directory](/integrations/integrations-directory) for what counts as standard vs. premium.
- **Available plans** — the tiers cfo.ai offers and what each includes.

Ari reports AI usage as a percentage and money as dollar amounts, since that's how the workspace's own billing data is expressed.

AI usage covers Ari's own model calls and the images Ari generates in a chat when you ask for one; a higher-quality image costs more. A workspace with no spendable usage left gets the usual pause before any image is requested.

## Plans

Open **Settings → Billing** to see the plans and switch between them. The plan cards show the current prices and limits for your workspace; the shape of each plan is:

- **Free** — start with your own data. It comes with a one-time allotment of AI credits that does not replenish, accepts CSV uploads as the only data source, and needs no card.
- **Starter** — build and run the finance model. Standard AI usage that resets monthly, five standard integrations, a ceiling on synced rows, the MCP server and Slack app, proactive alerts and Triggers, unlimited Models and Scenarios, and one dedicated workspace.
- **Growth** — everything in Starter with a multiple of the AI usage, more integrations, and a higher synced-row ceiling.
- **Enterprise** — custom pricing and AI credits. Adds premium integrations, including NetSuite and data warehouses, multiple workspaces, SSO / SAML, priority support, and a dedicated Forward Deployed Finance Partner with a shared Slack channel. The card opens a conversation with sales rather than switching the plan.

If your card is declined when you add it or pay, the form repeats the card provider's reason and adds **Check your payment details or try a different card.**

Use the **Monthly** / **Annual** toggle to compare cadences. Annual is selected by default when you first choose a paid plan and shows the saving against monthly billing. A paid plan starts with a 14-day free trial: select **Start free trial** on the card. Nothing is charged when the trial starts, and the card shows the price that applies per month after the trial. When you sign up, the plan page says **$0 today. Cancel anytime before it ends and you won’t be charged, and you still get all 14 days free.** The **Add a payment method** dialog names the date to cancel by. Canceling during the trial does not cut it short.

Plans differ in how many connections of each class you can hold, not in what a connection does. File uploads never count toward the integration limit, and neither do the API and MCP connections Ari calls live; see [API and MCP connections do not sync](/integrations/integrations-directory#api-and-mcp-connections-do-not-sync).

## Usage settings

**Settings → Usage** shows your included AI usage, extra usage, data, and integrations, with three views for managing spend:

- **Spend limit** — a monthly cap on extra usage. Raise or remove it if a purchase is being blocked.
- **Buy credits** — add a one-time amount to your purchased usage balance.
- **Auto-reload** — keep the balance topped up automatically. Turn on **Auto-reload**, then set **When the balance falls below** an amount, **add** a reload amount, and **Save**. Auto-reload needs a default payment method in Billing first. The balance line reads **Auto-reload on**, **off**, or **paused**; it pauses after a failed payment until you update the payment method or buy credits.

## Who can see and change billing

Every member of a workspace can open **Billing** and **Usage**, see the plan and the numbers, change the plan, buy credits, and edit the spend limit or auto-reload, because every member is an administrator. Some workspaces are billed outside the app and show no Billing section at all.

A plan is priced for the workspace, not for each person in it, so inviting another administrator does not change what you pay. What the plan buys — your included AI usage and your standard and premium connection slots — is shared by everyone in the workspace.

## Changing your plan or limits

Ask Ari where to go, and it will point you to the right section in Settings. Ari won't quote you a specific price for a plan change, since Settings shows the exact number at confirmation, and pricing can vary by workspace.

When you choose your first paid plan, the plan cards open on **Annual** pricing. The toggle names the saving, such as **Annual — save 20%**; switch it to **Monthly** to see month-to-month prices.

## During your trial

While a workspace is on a trial and has not paid its first invoice, a banner above the workspace reads **Need a hand with cfo.ai?** with a **Talk to our Forward Deployed team** link to book a call. Select the **Dismiss** control (the ✕ at the right) to hide the banner for the rest of the day; it returns the next day until the first invoice is paid.

If you cancel during the trial, the trial still runs to its end, and the workspace then returns to the plan chooser rather than closing. The paid plans there start without another trial, because a workspace gets one trial.

### Start the paid plan before the trial ends

There is one self-serve way to end a trial early, and it appears only after the workspace has used all the AI usage included with the trial. A banner above the workspace then reads **You’ve used all the AI usage included with your trial** and carries a **Start paid plan** button. Select it, confirm, and cfo.ai ends the trial, charges the card on file, and refreshes your included AI usage for a new billing cycle. **Settings → Billing** has no separate control for this, so a workspace that still has trial usage left waits for the trial to end on its own.

Dismissing that banner hides it for the rest of the trial, not only for the day. If you dismissed it and still want to start the paid plan now, select **Support** in the navigation at the top of these docs.

## Cancel your subscription

There is no way to pause a subscription and pick it up later. Canceling is the only stop. It stops the billing and leaves your data in place rather than deleting the Model, and when the term ends the workspace returns to the plan chooser until someone picks a plan again. Deleting a workspace or an account is a separate request; see [Workspace and team settings](/workspace-settings/team-and-workspace).

Open **Settings → Billing**, find **Cancel subscription**, and select **Cancel**. The dialog asks why you are canceling and names the date through which service runs: a paid plan stays active until the end of the current plan term and is not charged again, and a trial stays active until it ends, with no charge.

You can undo the cancellation any time before that date. Open the plan chooser in **Settings → Billing** and keep your current plan or choose another one; either one calls the cancellation off.

## If AI usage runs out

When the workspace has no spendable AI usage remaining, Ari explains the account state and further requests are paused until usage is restored.

A workspace administrator can review the balance, purchase additional usage when available, or adjust auto-reload and monthly spending settings. A teammate without billing access should contact a workspace administrator.

If a monthly spending cap is preventing a purchase, adjust the applicable limit in Usage settings before trying again.
