Ari is your AI finance coworker. Start with a decision, such as whether you can hire, when cash gets tight, or why expenses changed. Ari can build the Model behind the answer using connected data, an uploaded file, or clearly stated assumptions.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your workspace">
    Sign in at [app.cfo.ai](https://app.cfo.ai) with **Continue with Google**, **Sign in with Microsoft**, or your work email: enter it, select **Send code**, and type the six-digit code from the email. **Continue with Google** and **Sign in with Microsoft** show a spinner once selected and take no further clicks for 10 seconds while the provider's page opens. cfo.ai sends one address at most five codes, and checks at most ten, in 15 minutes; past that, the sign-in page answers **Too many requests** until the window passes. A code works once, for 10 minutes. When a code is refused, the page says why: **That code does not match the one that was sent.**, **That code expired. A code works for 10 minutes after it is sent.**, **That code was already used. A code works once.**, or **Too many wrong codes were entered, so that code no longer works.** When more than one code was sent to your address in those 10 minutes, a wrong code reads **That code was not accepted. [N] codes were sent to this address in the last 10 minutes, and only the newest one works.** with the actual count. **Continue** stays disabled while the boxes hold a refused code; change a digit or select **Resend code**. If you first saw cfo.ai through a demo someone shared with you, signing up gives you a workspace of your own, and you keep your access to the demo. Your workspace holds your Model, Pages, Scenarios, data connections, and team settings.

    During onboarding, a new workspace opens in a chat named **Welcome**, with Ari's greeting and a starting card. cfo.ai starts researching you as soon as you verify your email, with a public search for the email address. For a work address, cfo.ai also searches for the company behind its domain. Ari posts a short company brief in the chat when the research finishes; while it is still running, the chat shows **Searching the web** in its place. The brief ends with a **Sources** list: your signup's email domain, then each public page the research used, linked by its site and labeled with what ties it to you, such as **shows your full email address**, **on your email domain**, **found by searching your email domain**, or **matches your name only**. For a personal email, such as a Gmail address, a page that matches only your name is left out, so the brief does not ask whether a stranger's profile is yours. Confirm or correct the brief. That context helps Ari choose a sensible starting point. The brief is researched for the person who created the workspace; a teammate who joins the workspace later sees the greeting and the starting card without a brief.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask a business question">
    Describe the decision that matters. You do not need to know which financial model or report to request.

    > Can we hire three engineers in September? Show me what happens to payroll, cash, and cash runway.

    Ari can use the current company context to determine which information and calculations the answer needs. For more examples, see [Ari prompts](/ari/prompts).

  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to begin">
    The starting card beside Ari's greeting asks where to start, and your first answer picks its follow-up question. It does not wait for the company brief. Answer and select **Get started** to send your answers to Ari: sending them starts your first Model or, when you ask for your real data, the steps that lead to it. The card's footer has **Dismiss** and **Get started**, and an attach-file button, **Attach files to your answer**, appears beside them when your answer can take files: when you choose **Upload an existing model**, or when you are typing an answer of your own. Files sent without text read as **See attached files**. **Dismiss** sends the answers you have already picked and declines only the open questions.

    - **Where would you like to start?** Choose **My business** to plan the company Ari researched or **Something hypothetical** to explore another one. **Other…** lets you type a longer answer and attach files.
    - **What should I work from?** appears when you chose **My business** and decides what your first Model is built from. Choose **Connect my data** when accounting, HR, CRM, spreadsheet, or database history should inform the answer: a source whose data is ready is read now; Ari waits for a connection or uploaded file that is still loading; and when nothing is connected yet, Ari opens the source catalog so you can choose and authorize one. In both waiting cases Ari replies briefly with where your data stands and one question about what you most want to see first, and builds your first forecast from your records once they arrive. Choose **Upload an existing model** to hand Ari a workbook, or **I want to type or upload context** to describe the business in your own words, with files if you like. Dismissing this question chooses estimates as the source of the numbers, as its note **Dismiss this to start from estimates and what you tell me.** says. When research or your answers established which business you run, Ari builds a full first Model from a business description and labeled assumptions; when nothing has, for example a signup with a personal email address and no company named, Ari first asks what the business does. If you ask for an estimate while a connection is still loading, Ari builds the estimate.
    - **Which business should I use?** appears when you chose **Something hypothetical**. Choose **A business like mine** or **Another business, I'll choose** and name it. A name everyone ties to one well-known company is that company; any other name may be a small business that shares it or your own idea, so Ari asks which you mean before building.
    - **Import an existing model** when a workbook or report already contains the financial structure you want to rebuild: choose **Upload an existing model** on the card and attach the file with the button that appears, or attach it to the chat with your own message.

    Bring your own numbers when you can: connect your accounting system, attach your current model, or describe how the business makes money. Estimates work for a first look, but Ari builds a Model that fits your business when it starts from your data. See [Your first 10 minutes](/getting-started/first-10-minutes) for a prompt for each option. The sidebar's **Model** section, with the Scenario picker and the list of Pages, appears once Ari creates your first Page.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the first result">
    Ari names the Pages your business needs, then builds them one at a time, so you can read the first Page while the next one forms; a forecast simulator and a walkthrough of the model follow the Pages. Review Ari's answer and check the sources, assumptions, date range, and business categories. If Ari creates a Page or Table Block, open it. Ask Ari to explain a number or adjust an assumption, or change the Model directly.

    > Show me the hiring assumptions, then move the start dates to October and compare the cash forecast.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide what comes next">
    Add another source, create a Scenario, prepare a report, or save a recurring workflow. You and Ari keep working from the same Model, so the answer remains visible and useful after the first conversation.
  </Step>
</Steps>
