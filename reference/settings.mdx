Settings holds the workspace's options and your own preferences. To open it, select your name or picture at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Settings**. Settings opens on **General**, with the sections listed down the left side in this order.

| Section | Who sees it | What it holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **General** | Everyone | The workspace name and brand, date defaults and Last close, and your appearance |
| **Workspace** | Administrators | Inviting, removing, and listing administrators |
| **Profile** | Everyone | Your name and sign-in email |
| **Ari** | Everyone | Ari's thinking animation, sound, notifications, and Slack |
| **Connections** | Everyone, once the workspace has started its trial or a plan | The cfo.ai MCP server address, and the MCP servers and APIs Ari can call |
| **Skills** | Everyone | Your workspace's saved Skills and the optional built-in Skills |
| **Billing** | Workspaces billed in the app | Plan, payment methods, billing history, and cancellation |
| **Usage** | Workspaces billed in the app | AI usage, extra usage, spend limit, auto-reload, and integration slots |

Every member of a workspace is an administrator, so everyone sees **Workspace**. A workspace billed outside the app has no **Billing** or **Usage** section.

## General

### Workspace name

The **Workspace** section of **General** holds the workspace's name.

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | The workspace's name. **Save** saves a new name. Renaming also changes the workspace's web address: bookmarks to the old address stop working, and share links keep working. |

| Message | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Workspace renamed** / **Your workspace is now “[name]”.** | The new name is saved. |
| **Couldn’t rename workspace** | The rename failed; the reason follows. |
| **Only admins can change the name.** | You cannot change the name. |
| **I’m finding a better name for your workspace — you can pick one yourself anytime.** | cfo.ai is naming a new workspace from your company's details. |

### Brand

A brand gives the workspace a name, logo, colors, and heading typeface that everyone who opens it sees, including people reading a shared Page. See [Brand your workspace](/guides/brand-your-workspace).

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Website address** and **Build from a website** | Shown with no brand. Opens a chat in which Ari builds a brand from the website you typed. |
| **Set up by hand** | Shown with no brand. Opens the brand form. |
| Brand form | **Name** (up to 40 characters), **Logo**, **Logo for dark mode**, **Light mode colors** and **Dark mode colors** (**Page surface**, **Panel surface**, **Text**, **Accent**), **Heading typeface** (**cfo.ai default**, **Source Serif**, **Newsreader**, **Fraunces**, **Zilla Slab**, or **DM Sans**), and **Corner shape** (**Square**, **Soft**, or **Round**), with **Save** and **Cancel**. |
| **Edit** | Shown with a brand. Opens the brand form. |
| **Copy to other workspaces** | Shown with a brand. Copies it to workspaces you choose. |
| **Remove brand** | Shown with a brand. After you confirm, everyone sees their own theme again. |

Someone who cannot change the brand sees **This workspace uses the** [name] **brand. Workspace admins set the brand.**

### Date and time

The section is headed **Date & Time**. Its settings belong to the Scenario you are viewing in the sidebar's Scenario picker. A Scenario follows Main's settings until someone changes them in that Scenario. See [Set Last close and date defaults](/guides/set-dates-and-last-close).

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Default date granularity** | The period new tables show: **Raw**, **Day**, **Week**, **Month**, **Quarter**, **Half**, or **Year**. **Month** unless changed. Last close snaps to the end of a period at this granularity. |
| **Default date range** | The first and last dates new tables show. A new workspace starts from January 1 of the year six months ago through December 31 of the year six months ahead. Existing tables keep their own range. |
| **Last close** | The end of the last period you closed, your month-end. Periods up to it are Actuals, and later periods are Forecast. Pick a period; cfo.ai saves its last day. A new workspace starts at the last day of the previous calendar month, and Last close never moves on its own. See [Actuals, Forecast, and Last close](/concepts/actuals-and-forecast). |

There is no fiscal-year setting: quarters, half-years, and years follow the calendar year, January to December. See [Fiscal years](/concepts/actuals-and-forecast#fiscal-years-quarters-and-years-follow-the-calendar).

| Message | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Loading…** / **Couldn’t load** | Shown in **Last close** while it loads, or when it cannot be read. |
| **Failed to save date settings** | The change was not saved; the settings return to what is saved. |
| **Failed to load date settings** | The settings could not be read. Reopen Settings. |

### Appearance

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Color mode** | Light or dark mode: **Auto**, **Light**, or **Dark**. **Auto** follows your device's setting. Saved in this browser. The account menu's **Appearance** item sets the same thing. |
| **Theme** | **Warm paper**, cfo.ai's own theme, **Prism**, **Pid Girl**, **Hot Dog Stand**, **50s CFO**, **Columnar Pad**, **Spreadsheet**, or **Custom**. Saved to your account and seen only by you. |
| **Create a custom theme** box and **Personalize** | Shown when **Theme** is **Custom**. **Personalize** opens a chat where Ari builds a theme from what you typed. See [Change your theme, sounds, and notifications](/guides/personalize-cfoai). |

In a workspace with a brand, **Theme** is replaced by **This workspace uses the** [name] **brand. Your own theme applies in workspaces without a brand.** If a theme change cannot be saved, a notice reads **Failed to save theme**.

## Workspace

### Invite admin

**Invite admin** adds a teammate to the workspace. The section reads **Admins can see and edit all pages in the workspace, invite others, and manage billing and other settings.** See [Invite or remove teammates](/guides/invite-teammates).

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Email address** | The address to invite. |
| **Add admin** | Creates the invitation and emails it. The form then reads **Admin invitation created for** [address]. Adding an address that already has a pending invitation sends the email again. An address that already belongs to the workspace is refused. |

cfo.ai has no other role to invite someone to: every invitation is for an administrator. For read-only access, use a share link; see [Share a Page outside your workspace](/guides/share-a-page).

### Admins

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Ask Ari** | Starts a chat in which Ari lists the people in the workspace and the pending invitations, then asks what you want to change. Ari changes nothing until you answer. |
| A row | A person's name and email, marked **(you)** for you, with **Admin** for someone who joined or **Invited** for a pending invitation. |
| **Remove member** | On an **Admin** row's menu. Opens **Remove member?**: **They will lose access to this workspace. Any pending invitations for this email will also be cancelled. Their other workspaces are unaffected.** Your own row has no menu. |
| **Cancel invitation** | On an **Invited** row's menu. Opens **Cancel invitation?**: **They will no longer be able to join through this invitation. If they have already joined, their membership stays unchanged.** |

| Message | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| [email] **was removed from this workspace.** | The person was removed, and the list reloaded. |
| [email] **invitation was cancelled.** | The invitation was cancelled, and the list reloaded. |
| **Couldn’t load the admins of this workspace. Please try again.** | The list could not load. |

## Profile

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile** | Your picture, from the account you sign in with. |
| **Name** | Your name. **Edit** opens the field, and **Save** saves it. |
| **Email** | The email you sign in with. **Edit** opens **Change email**, with **New email** and **Send verification code**, then a **Verification code** field under **Verify your new email** for the code sent to the new address. cfo.ai may also ask for a **Sign-in code** under **Verify it’s you**. Your email changes only after the new address is verified. |

| Message | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Name updated** / **Could not update name** | The name change was saved, or it failed. |
| **Email updated** | The new email is verified and saved. |

## Ari

The sections appear in this order.

### Thinking animation

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Show Ari's edits as they happen** | On by default. **Ari points at each change on the page and explains it in a short note. When off, only the finished edits are marked.** Saved in this browser. See [Review Ari's formula changes](/guides/review-ari-formula-changes). |

### Sound

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Play sound when Ari is done** | Plays a short sound when Ari finishes a reply. |
| Volume | **Quiet**, **Normal**, or **Louder**. |
| **Alert sound** | **Default** or **Beep Me**. |

Each change plays the sound. These settings are saved in this browser.

### Notifications

Shown in browsers that support notifications.

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Notify me when Ari finishes in this browser** | Shows a browser notification when Ari replies, or needs your answer, in a chat you are not looking at. **Applies to this browser only. Ari stays quiet while you’re looking at the chat.** |

When the browser blocks notifications, the switch is unavailable and reads **Notifications are blocked for cfo.ai. Allow them in your browser’s site settings to turn this on.** For the notifications themselves, see [Chat controls and messages](/reference/chat#notifications-when-ari-finishes).

### Slack

| Status | What the section shows |
| --- | --- |
| Not connected | **Add Ari to Slack** with **Add to Slack** and **Enable messages to and from Ari via DM.** While Slack's sign-in window is open, the button reads **Cancel**. |
| Connected | **Connected to** [Slack workspace] and **Ari can now DM you in this Slack workspace.**, with **Disconnect**. |
| Disconnecting | **Disconnect "[Slack workspace]"?** with **cfo.ai Agent will no longer be able to post to this workspace until you reconnect.** |

Anyone whose Slack profile email matches a member of the workspace can message Ari in Slack, and Ari works as that person, in direct messages only. See [Talk to Ari from Slack](/guides/use-ari-in-slack).

## Connections

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Connect via MCP** | The cfo.ai MCP server address, `https://api.cfo.ai/mcp`, with **Copy cfo.ai MCP URL**. AI tools such as Claude or Codex connect to it and sign in through the browser; cfo.ai issues no API key. See [Connect Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, or another AI tool to cfo.ai](/guides/connect-an-ai-client). |
| **Add connections** | Tabs **MCP** and **APIs** for other services whose tools Ari can call. Each tab offers **Add manually** and **Add with Ari**. See [Give Ari access to another service with MCP or an API](/guides/give-ari-another-service). |

While Ari cannot be used, for example when the workspace is out of AI usage, **Add with Ari** is unavailable, and a line reads **Add with Ari is unavailable.** followed by the reason. Data sources such as QuickBooks are connected from **Connections** in the sidebar; see [Connect QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite, or Rillet](/guides/connect-accounting).

## Skills

The section reads **Teach Ari workflows you repeat. Your skills go with you to every workspace you work in.** Your Skills belong to you: every workspace where you are a member shows the same list, and nobody else sees it. See [Save a workflow as a Skill](/guides/save-a-skill).

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Write manually** | Opens the Skill editor, headed **New skill**. |
| **Build with Ari** | Opens a new chat that starts with **Help me build a reusable skill.** Unavailable while Ari cannot be used. |
| **Search skills** | Filters both lists by name and description. |

### Your skills

Each of your Skills shows its name, its description, where it applies, **Edit**, and **Delete**. The list includes Skills limited to your other workspaces. With none saved, the list reads **No skills yet. Build one with Ari by describing a workflow, pasting instructions, or attaching a Markdown file.** The heading **Your skills** shows when the workspace offers built-in Skills below it.

| Where it applies | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **All your workspaces** | Ari follows the Skill in every workspace where you are a member. |
| **Only in** [workspace] | Ari follows the Skill only in that workspace. |
| **Not in use: you're no longer a member** | The Skill is limited to a workspace you left. Ari follows it there again if you rejoin. You can still edit it, change **Use in**, or delete it. |

| Editor field | Note in the editor |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | **Lowercase letters, digits, and hyphens. The name can't be changed later.** |
| **Description** | **Phrase it as an activation trigger ("Use when…") — Ari uses it to decide when the skill applies.** |
| **Instructions** | **Markdown. The full workflow, steps, and conventions Ari should follow when the skill activates.** |

**Use in** offers **All your workspaces**, the default for a new Skill, and **Only in** one workspace: the Skill's own workspace when it already has a limit, otherwise the workspace you are in. The editor saves with **Create skill** for a new Skill and **Save** for an existing one. **Delete** opens **Delete** [name]**?** with **Ari will stop using this skill in all your workspaces.** or, for a Skill limited to one workspace, **Ari will stop using this skill in** [workspace]**.**

| Message | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Name must be 1-64 lowercase letters or digits, with single hyphens between them (e.g. "board-report").** | The name breaks the naming rule. |
| **Description is required.** / **Instructions are required.** | A field is empty. |
| **Description must be at most 1024 characters.** / **Instructions must be at most 49,152 characters.** | A field is too long. |
| **A skill with this name already exists** | One of your Skills that applies here uses the name, or it is a built-in Skill's name. |
| **You have another skill with this name in another workspace. Choose another name, or keep this one in one workspace.** | A Skill for all your workspaces cannot share a name with one of your Skills limited to a workspace. |
| **You can have at most 100 skills. Delete one first.** | You have 100 Skills across all your workspaces. |
| **Only members of a workspace can save skills there** | You are a guest in this workspace, or you chose a workspace you are not a member of. |
| **Failed to load skills** / **Failed to delete skill** | The list or a deletion failed. Try again. |

### Built-in skills

The section reads **Enable ready-made workflows for everyone in this workspace. Skills stay up to date with improvements from cfo.ai.** Each optional built-in Skill has a switch: **Model audit**, **Flash report**, and **Model migration tie-out**. A switch applies to everyone in the workspace. Built-in Skills cannot be edited. See [Turn on a built-in Skill](/guides/turn-on-built-in-skills).

## Billing and Usage

**Billing** shows **Current plan**, **Payment methods**, **Billing history**, and **Cancel subscription**. **Usage** shows **Included plan usage**, **Extra usage**, and **Integrations**. Every field and message is on [Plans and billing](/reference/plans-and-billing).

## What Settings has no setting for

- A fiscal year that starts in a month other than January. See [Fiscal years](/concepts/actuals-and-forecast#fiscal-years-quarters-and-years-follow-the-calendar).
- A view-only or other lower role, access to single Pages for one person, or user groups. Use a share link for read-only access; see [Sharing and access](/concepts/sharing-and-access).
- An API key or access token for cfo.ai. AI tools connect to the MCP server and sign in through the browser; see [Is there a cfo.ai API or API key?](/guides/connect-an-ai-client#is-there-a-cfoai-api-or-api-key)
- Deleting a workspace or an account. See [Cancel your subscription or delete your data](/guides/cancel-or-delete).
- Pausing a subscription. See [Plans and billing](/reference/plans-and-billing#rules-and-limits).
- Moving Last close automatically. Ask Ari, or schedule a Trigger that does it; see [Schedule a recurring report with a Trigger](/guides/schedule-a-trigger).
- Folders or groups for workspaces. See [Create a second workspace or switch workspaces](/guides/manage-workspaces).
