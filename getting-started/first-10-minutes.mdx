Your first session has one goal: a Model of your own business that you have checked at least once. Ari does the building. You bring the facts only you have, such as your books, your current spreadsheet, or how the company makes money, and you correct Ari where it guessed.

That second part matters. Ari builds a closer fit when it starts from your numbers instead of estimates, and every correction you make in the first session carries into later answers.

## Before you begin

Have one of these ready:

- **Your accounting login**, such as QuickBooks Online, Xero, or NetSuite. You need to be an administrator of the company file, because you approve the connection yourself.
- **Your current model**, such as an Excel workbook.
- **A few facts about the business**, if you have neither: what you sell, what you charge, how many customers and employees you have, and how much cash is in the bank.

Set aside about 10 minutes. A large workbook or a first sync of many years of history can take longer, and you can keep working while it finishes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start from your business">
    A new workspace opens in a chat named **Welcome**. Ari posts a short brief about your company, researched from your work email's domain.

    - Read the brief and correct anything wrong, such as what you sell, who buys it, or how you charge. This is your first contribution to the Model.
    - On the starting card, choose **My business**.

    **Just show me what you can do** builds a small sample model. It is a fine tour, but it is not your business, so come back to **My business** when you are ready.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring something of your own">
    Pick the one that matches what you have today. Each takes a few minutes.

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Connect your books">
        Use this when your accounting system holds your history. Ask Ari:

        > Connect [QuickBooks Online / Xero / NetSuite] so we can build a monthly profit and loss statement.

        Ari opens the sign-in. You approve access in your browser; Ari cannot do that step for you. Ari tells you in the chat when the first load finishes and which months arrived. You can keep going with the next step while it loads.

        See [Connect and sync your data](/integrations/connect-and-sync-data) for other sources, such as payroll or a CRM.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Upload your model">
        Use this when you already plan in a spreadsheet. Attach the Excel workbook to the chat and ask:

        > Import this workbook into cfo.ai. Rebuild [the revenue forecast, hiring plan, and operating expenses] and keep the same assumptions.

        Ari reads the tabs, formulas, and values, then rebuilds them as Pages in your Model. A large workbook takes longer than 10 minutes. See [Import an existing spreadsheet model](/getting-started/import-a-spreadsheet-model).
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Describe your business">
        Use this when you have no system or model to share yet. Write a few lines in your own words:

        > We sell [product] to [customers] for [price] per [month / year / unit]. We have [number] customers and [number] employees. Our biggest costs are [costs]. We have [amount] in the bank.

        Ari labels every number you did not give it as an assumption, so you can replace it later. Connect your books when you can.
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask for your first Model">
    Say what you want to decide, not which report to build. For example:

    > Build a model of our business for the next [12 / 18 / 24] months. I want to know [how long our cash lasts / whether we can hire / when we break even].

    For a larger build, Ari may show a plan with the assumptions it intends to use. Change any that are wrong, then select **Build it**. Ari builds Pages one at a time, so you can read the first Page while the next one forms.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Check one number you already know">
    Open the first Page Ari built. Find a number you know by heart, such as last month's revenue, payroll, or cash in the bank.

    Select that cell and press **⌘ L** (Ctrl+L on Windows and Linux) to mention it in the chat, then ask:

    > Where does this number come from? Show me the source and the formula.

    If it is wrong, tell Ari what the right number is and why. That correction is worth more than any prompt.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Change one assumption">
    Pick an assumption you care about and change it, or ask Ari to:

    > Move the start date for the next two hires to [month] and show what happens to cash.

    The calculation engine recalculates every connected number. Ari writes formulas; it does not do the math in the chat.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## What done looks like

After your first session, you should have:

- At least one Page built from your own data, file, or description.
- One number you checked against a figure you trust.
- One assumption you changed, and the result you saw.

## If something looks wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The numbers do not match my books">
    Ask Ari to compare the Model with the source for one closed month and list every account that differs. A common cause is that the connection is still loading, or that the Model's last closed month is earlier than you expect.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Ari built something different from what I wanted">
    Select **Stop**, then say what you wanted instead. Changes Ari already saved stay in place, so ask what it finished before you continue. See [Steer, stop, and approve during a chat](/ari/during-a-conversation).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The reply stopped partway">
    Select **Retry** on **Ari stopped before finishing**. Anything Ari saved before the stop is still in the Model.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
