A conversation with Ari is not a single question and answer. Ari often works for several minutes, asks for details, proposes a plan, or needs your approval before it calls another service. This page covers the controls you have while that is happening.

## Stop Ari

While Ari is working, the send button becomes a **Stop** button. Select it when Ari is heading in the wrong direction or you want to change the request. Changes Ari already saved stay in place, so check the Page or ask Ari what it completed before you continue.

## Send a message while Ari is working

A message you send while Ari is idle starts a turn at once. It appears in the chat as a sent message, and the composer keeps focus, so you can type your next message while Ari works. Until Ari's first step shows, a **Thinking…** row holds the turn's place under your message; Ari's first reasoning or action replaces it.

You can type while Ari works. A message sent during a turn is **Queued for after this turn**: Ari finishes what it is doing, then reads your message. Use this to add a detail you forgot or to redirect the next step. You can attach a file while Ari works too, including a screenshot you paste or drop into the composer, and send it with or without text; the same five-file limit applies.

Hover a queued message to edit or delete it before Ari reaches it. Two kinds of queued message cannot be edited, only deleted: a plan approval and a set of answers to Ari's questions. A message that started a turn cannot be edited or deleted; it is already on its way to Ari.

## Follow Ari's activity

Ari's reasoning and the actions it takes collect in an **Activity** group under your message. The group opens while Ari works and stays open as long as it is on your screen, so the rows never vanish while you read them. Once you scroll past it, it collapses out of view and stays collapsed when you scroll back. Your own click to open or close a group always wins, and groups loaded from an earlier conversation start collapsed.

The log does not report tool errors. Ari often recovers from a failed step on its own, by retrying or reading the data another way, so a step whose call failed reads as a finished step with the normal check mark, and the collapsed summary folds it into **and N more** instead of naming it. Anything you need to know about a failure is in Ari's reply. A step you stopped yourself still reads **Cancelled**, and **Conversation interrupted** keeps its red mark.

Work that runs beside the conversation, such as a sync Ari started, shows as a line of text above the message box, under Ari's **Thinking…** status line; select the line to open its card. Press the Up arrow from the start of your draft to reach the line nearest the message box, and Down to return to the draft. See [Follow a sync from the chat](/integrations/connect-and-sync-data#follow-a-sync-from-the-chat).

## Watch Ari edit the page

When Ari edits a table you are looking at, it replays each edit on the page: a caption moves to the target, the editor opens, the new value or formula is typed in, and the changed cells are highlighted. The caption's first line is Ari's own note for the change, such as **Setting upside churn assumptions**. Its second line says what changed: `Churn [Starter] 5% → 4%` for a value that replaced a value, `set to 4%` for a new value, `cleared (was $15,000)` for a removed one, or the new formula followed by `(was …)`. Filling many cells with one rule reads `Filling 24 cells in Revenue with …`. While a change is still running, the caption shows the note alone and adds **Still working** after about eight seconds.

A text block Ari rewrites while you have its Page open updates in place, without a reload, and the update does not enter your undo history. While you have unsaved typing in that block, from the first keystroke until your save is confirmed, Ari's change is not shown; your save then replaces the block, because the last save wins.

The first edit of each kind in a turn plays at full speed with a short explanation; repeated edits of the same kind play at twice the speed. Rest the pointer on the caption to pause the playback. If your system asks for reduced motion, the captions and highlights appear without movement.

While Ari works, the page follows it to each table it edits. Scrolling the page by about a third of its visible height stops following; a smaller scroll, a click, or typing only pauses it for a moment. A pill near the bottom of the page then reads **Stopped following · Click to follow Ari** (**Tap** on a touch screen) for a few seconds and then **Ari is working…**; the same pill appears when Ari is building the page you have open somewhere out of view. Select the pill to scroll to Ari's newest edit and follow again; you can also drag it to another spot, and the spot is remembered. While you are not following, a page Ari opens from its work is held and opens when you follow again. Following does not resume on its own.

## Answer Ari's questions

When Ari needs a few details before it can continue, it asks them as a short form in the chat. Fill in the answers you know and select **Dismiss** on any question you would rather not answer; Ari continues without it and the transcript marks that question as **Declined**. **Undo** restores a dismissed question before you send.

When the form has several questions, choosing the answer to a question that takes one choice settles it and opens the next unanswered question. The button at the bottom of the form reads **Next** while a later question remains, and selecting it moves you to that question whether or not the one on screen is answered. On the last question it reads **Submit answers** (**Submit** on a one-question form) and stays off until every question is answered or dismissed. A question that takes several choices stays open until you move on, because another choice may still be intended.

Your answers appear in the transcript as a record of the form, each question above the answer you gave.

## Approve an email Ari drafts

Ari emails you at your verified address as soon as you ask. An email to anyone else does not go out on its own: Ari posts a preview card in the chat, headed **Preview**, with the recipients, subject, and body it would send, and nothing leaves cfo.ai until you press **Send**. Select **Edit** to change the draft, including the recipient list, where addresses separated by commas or Enter become removable pills; a message can carry up to ten recipients. If the mail provider accepts only some of the addresses, the card keeps the rejected ones so you can send to them again without emailing the others twice. A scheduled Trigger follows the same rule, so a run that addresses someone other than you stops at the preview.

## Approve a plan

For a larger piece of work, Ari may present a plan with the assumptions it intends to use. Each assumption offers Ari's suggested choices plus **Other**, where you type your own value. Select **Build it** to start; the plan is sent back to Ari as a message that lists the assumptions you selected, so the record of what you approved stays in the conversation.

Ari cannot start until every assumption that it marked as required has a value.

While Ari works through a plan, its plan card in the transcript lists the steps with a progress count such as **2/5**. When Ari recorded the decisions the plan rests on, an **Assumptions** group under the steps names each one with the choice Ari made, so you can check them without reading the whole transcript.

## Reply to part of an answer

Ari's answers can run to several paragraphs, a list, or a table. To ask about one part of an answer, select the words in that answer instead of retyping them. A **Reply** control appears above the selection. Select it, or press **⌘ L** (Ctrl+L on Windows and Linux), and the quoted passage lands in the chat input as an excerpt block beside any attached files. Type your question and send it. The sent message shows the excerpt above what you typed, and Ari reads the quote as an attributed excerpt of its own words.

A message can carry up to five excerpts of up to 2,000 characters each. Send stays off until you write something to go with them, because a bare quote gives Ari nothing to act on. Click an excerpt, in the input or in the transcript, to scroll to the original words and highlight them. Reply is not offered while the answer is still streaming, when the selection spans two answers, or in a read-only chat.

## Download a file Ari made

When Ari makes a file for you, such as a workbook export, an image, or a document it delivers, the file shows as a download card directly under the answer that ends the turn, where you finish reading, rather than above a long answer. Cards for plans, approvals, Pages, and Scenarios keep their place above the answer. When the answer names the file by its exact file name, with the same letter case and extension, that name is a link to the same download; a name inside a code block, or spelled differently, stays plain text.

## Approve a tool call

When Ari wants to call a connected external service, such as an MCP connector or an API connection from **Settings → Connections**, it pauses and shows the call for approval. Choose one of:

- **Approve** to run this one call.
- **Always allow** to let Ari run this tool without asking again for the whole workspace. Only someone who can manage workspace settings sees this option. Once the workspace confirms the choice, every other card waiting for the same tool on the same server reads **Always allowed** and those calls run on Ari's next turn; a card for another tool, or for a tool of the same name on another server or API, still asks.
- **Decline** to skip the call. Ari continues without it.

Anyone in the chat can approve or decline the single call. After you choose, Ari continues on its own; you do not need to send another message.

## When a reply is interrupted

If Ari's reply is cut off, the transcript shows **Ari stopped before finishing** with a **Retry** button. Select it to have Ari pick the work back up. cfo.ai also tries on its own to continue a reply that was interrupted, for example when the service running Ari restarted. When every attempt fails, the reply ends with **I stopped before finishing this reply. It was interrupted, and repeated attempts to continue it did not finish. Changes I saved before the interruption are kept. Sending the message again starts a new reply.** Anything Ari saved before the interruption is still in the Model. If your connection drops, the chat says **No internet connection. Reconnect to continue with Ari.** When the service running Ari loses touch with cfo.ai for a moment, the chat reads **Ari hit a temporary connection issue. Retrying now.** and Ari continues from the steps it already saved; if the trouble does not clear, the reply ends with **Ari hit a temporary connection problem and could not recover. Please try again in a moment.**

When billing stops a turn, the reply ends with Ari's own explanation, which begins **I had to stop here** and names the cause and the fix, for example **I had to stop here: this workspace is out of AI usage. Buy more usage or upgrade the plan in Settings → Billing, then send a new message and I'll pick up where we left off.** The same shape covers a reached spend limit, a plan limit, a failed usage purchase, a trial not yet started, and a workspace with no active plan. No **Retry** is offered, because the same request would stop the same way: fix the cause in **Settings → Billing**, then send a new message. If more AI usage is already on the way, for example from auto-reload, the message says so and asks you to send a new message in a moment.

A very long conversation can outgrow what Ari can read in one request. The chat then says **This request is too large for Ari to continue. Start a new chat with a shorter request or fewer attachments.** and offers no **Retry**, because the same request would fail the same way. Start a new chat with **⌘ /** (Ctrl+/ on Windows and Linux) and restate what you need; attach a file again only if the new request needs it.

## Manage your chats

The chat sidebar lists your conversations under **My sessions**, and any conversation someone else opened for you under **Shared with me**. Filter the list by **Active**, **Archived**, or **All**. Both sections keep their order, newest activity first, when you open a chat; a chat moves up only when something happens in it.

The list starts compact, with your newest chats behind a **Show more** row. Once it is expanded, scrolling to the bottom loads older chats automatically; the list reads **Loading older chats…** while a page loads and offers **Retry loading chats** if one fails. There is no **Show less**: fold the **Chats** section and open it again to return to the compact list.

Open a conversation's menu to **Archive chat**, which hides it from the active list without deleting anything, or **Unarchive chat** to bring it back. A conversation you have not sent anything in yet offers **Discard chat** instead. You can also rename a conversation inline.

A conversation shared with you is read-only: the chat says **You can view this shared conversation, but can't send messages.** Start your own chat to ask Ari about what you see.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Shortcut | What it does |
|---|---|
| **⌘ /** (Ctrl+/ on Windows and Linux) | Start a new chat |
| **⌘ L** (Ctrl+L on Windows and Linux) | Mention the selected cell or block in the current chat, or reply to the text you selected in an Ari answer |
| **Enter** | Send, or queue the message if Ari is working |
