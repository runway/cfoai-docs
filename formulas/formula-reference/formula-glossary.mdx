---
title: "Formula glossary"
---

## Aggregation

The process of combining several values into one result, such as a sum or average.

## Date reference

A named point on the timeline. **Last close** commonly separates closed Actuals from open Forecast FormulaRanges.

## Dimension

A named category used to group, filter, or segment Variables. Examples include Department, Region, and Product.

## DimensionItem

One value within a Dimension, such as Sales within Department or West within Region.

## Expression

The part of a formula that describes what to calculate.

## Formula

A saved calculation for a Variable. A formula has an expression and a scope.

## FormulaRange

A span of the timeline that can use its own formula, such as Actuals or Forecast. Also called a formula period or formula break in older material; **FormulaRange** is the current canonical name.

## Scenario

A version of the model, such as Base or Plan. Formulas are saved within a scenario.

## Scope

The part of the model where a formula applies. Scope can include segmentations, segment values, Date, and FormulaRanges.

## Segment

A specific Dimension value or combination of values, such as Department = Sales.

## Segmentation

The Dimension or Dimensions used to break down a value, such as Revenue by Department.

## Variable

A named model quantity — a value you calculate, aggregate, or hold, such as Revenue, Headcount, or Gross Margin. A formula calculates a Variable's value.
