Ari can read your workspace's billing status directly, so you don't need to leave the conversation to check your plan or usage. Ari can't change billing itself — every answer points you to the specific Settings section to make the actual change.

## What Ari can tell you

Ask Ari about:

- **Plan** — your current plan, price, status, renewal date, and any scheduled change.
- **Usage** — your monthly AI usage as a percentage of the plan's allotment, your extra-usage balance, whether auto-reload is on, and your spending limit.
- **Integrations** — how many standard and premium connections your plan allows, and how many you're using. See [Integrations Directory](/integrations/integrations-directory) for what counts as standard vs. premium.
- **Available plans** — the tiers cfo.ai offers and what each includes.

Ari reports AI usage as a percentage and money as dollar amounts, since that's how the workspace's own billing data is expressed.

## Plans

Open **Settings → Billing** to see the plans and switch between them. The plan cards show the current prices and limits for your workspace; the shape of each plan is:

- **Free** — start with your own data. It comes with a one-time allotment of AI credits that does not replenish, accepts CSV uploads as the only data source, and needs no card.
- **Startup** — build and run the finance model. Standard AI usage that resets monthly, a set number of standard integrations, a ceiling on synced rows, the MCP server and Slack app, proactive alerts and Triggers, unlimited Models and Scenarios, and one dedicated workspace.
- **Growth** — everything in Startup with a multiple of the AI usage, more integrations, and a higher synced-row ceiling.
- **Enterprise** — custom pricing and AI credits. Adds premium integrations, including NetSuite and data warehouses, multiple workspaces, SSO / SAML, priority support, and a dedicated Forward Deployed Finance Partner with a shared Slack channel. The card opens a conversation with sales rather than switching the plan.

Use the **Monthly** / **Annual** toggle to compare cadences. Annual is selected by default when you first choose a paid plan and shows the saving against monthly billing. A paid plan starts with a free trial: select **Start free trial** on the card. Nothing is charged when the trial starts, and the card shows the price that applies per month after the trial.

Plans differ in how many connections of each class you can hold, not in what a connection does. File uploads never count toward the integration limit.

## Usage settings

**Settings → Usage** shows your included AI usage, extra usage, data, and integrations, with three views for managing spend:

- **Spend limit** — a monthly cap on extra usage. Raise or remove it if a purchase is being blocked.
- **Buy credits** — add a one-time amount to your purchased usage balance.
- **Auto-reload** — keep the balance topped up automatically. Turn on **Auto-reload**, then set **When the balance falls below** an amount, **add** a reload amount, and **Save**. Auto-reload needs a default payment method in Billing first. The balance line reads **Auto-reload on**, **off**, or **paused**; it pauses after a failed payment until you update the payment method or buy credits.

## Who can see and change billing

Every member of a workspace can open **Billing** and **Usage**, see the plan and the numbers, change the plan, buy credits, and edit the spend limit or auto-reload, because every member is an administrator. Some workspaces are billed outside the app and show no Billing section at all.

## Changing your plan or limits

Ask Ari where to go, and it will point you to the right section in Settings. Ari won't quote you a specific price for a plan change, since Settings shows the exact number at confirmation, and pricing can vary by workspace.

When you choose your first paid plan, the plan cards open on **Annual** pricing. The toggle names the saving, such as **Annual — save 20%**; switch it to **Monthly** to see month-to-month prices.

## During your trial

While a workspace is on a trial and has not paid its first invoice, a banner above the workspace reads **Need a hand with cfo.ai?** with a **Talk to our Forward Deployed team** link to book a call. Select the **Dismiss** control (the ✕ at the right) to hide the banner for the rest of the day; it returns the next day until the first invoice is paid.

## If AI usage runs out

When the workspace has no spendable AI usage remaining, Ari explains the account state and further requests are paused until usage is restored.

A workspace administrator can review the balance, purchase additional usage when available, or adjust auto-reload and monthly spending settings. A teammate without billing access should contact a workspace administrator.

If a monthly spending cap is preventing a purchase, adjust the applicable limit in Usage settings before trying again.
