Ari is your AI finance coworker. Start with a decision, such as whether you can hire, when cash gets tight, or why expenses changed. Ari can build the Model behind the answer using connected data, an uploaded file, or clearly stated assumptions.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your workspace">
    Sign in at [app.cfo.ai](https://app.cfo.ai) with **Continue with Google**, **Sign in with Microsoft**, or your work email: enter it, select **Send code**, and type the six-digit code from the email. Your workspace holds your Model, Pages, Scenarios, data connections, and team settings.

    During onboarding, a new workspace opens in a chat named **Welcome**, with Ari's greeting and a starting card. cfo.ai starts researching your company from your work email's domain as soon as you verify the email, and Ari posts a short company brief in the chat when that research finishes; while it is still running, the chat shows **Searching the web** in its place. Confirm or correct the brief. That context helps Ari choose a sensible starting point. The brief is researched for the person who created the workspace; a teammate who joins the workspace later sees the greeting and the starting card without a brief.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask a business question">
    Describe the decision that matters. You do not need to know which financial model or report to request.

    > Can we hire three engineers in September? Show me what happens to payroll, cash, and cash runway.

    Ari can use the current company context to determine which information and calculations the answer needs.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to begin">
    The starting card beside Ari's greeting asks where to start, and your first answer picks its follow-up question. It does not wait for the company brief. Answer and select **Get started** to send your answers to Ari: sending them starts your first Model or, when you ask for your real data, the steps that lead to it. The card's footer has an attach-file button next to **Dismiss** and **Get started**, so you can share a file with your answers; files sent without text read as **See attached files**.

    - **Where would you like to start?** Choose **My business** to plan the company Ari researched or **Something hypothetical** to explore another one. **Other…** lets you type a longer answer and attach files.
    - **What should I work from?** appears when you chose **My business** and decides what your first Model is built from. Choose **Connect my data** when accounting, HR, CRM, spreadsheet, or database history should inform the answer: a source whose data is ready is read now; Ari waits for a connection or uploaded file that is still loading; and when nothing is connected yet, Ari opens the source catalog so you can choose and authorize one. In both waiting cases Ari replies briefly with where your data stands and one question about what you most want to see first, and builds your first forecast from your records once they arrive. Choose **Upload an existing model** to hand Ari a workbook, or **I want to type or upload context** to describe the business in your own words, with files if you like. Dismiss this question, as its note says, and Ari estimates your numbers for now, building a full first Model from a business description and labeled assumptions. If you ask for an estimate while a connection is still loading, Ari builds the estimate.
    - **Which business should I use?** appears when you chose **Something hypothetical**. Choose **A business like mine** or **Another business, I'll choose** and name it.
    - **Import an existing model** when a workbook or report already contains the financial structure you want to rebuild: choose **Upload an existing model** on the card and attach the file, or attach it to the chat with your own message.

    Connecting data is optional. You can start with estimates and add the supporting systems later. The sidebar's **Model** section, with the Scenario picker and the list of Pages, appears once Ari creates your first Page.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the first result">
    Ari names the Pages your business needs, then builds them one at a time, so you can read the first Page while the next one forms; a forecast simulator and a walkthrough of the model follow the Pages. Review Ari's answer and check the sources, assumptions, date range, and business categories. If Ari creates a Page or Table Block, open it. Ask Ari to explain a number or adjust an assumption, or change the Model directly.

    > Show me the hiring assumptions, then move the start dates to October and compare the cash forecast.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide what comes next">
    Add another source, create a Scenario, prepare a report, or save a recurring workflow. You and Ari keep working from the same Model, so the answer remains visible and useful after the first conversation.
  </Step>
</Steps>
