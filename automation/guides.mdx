A **Guide** is a saved, interactive walkthrough that spotlights parts of your workspace, one step at a time, with a coachmark of explanatory text at each stop. Use a Guide to onboard a new team member, walk someone through an analysis, or narrate a feature.

## Guide modes

- **Tour** — a step-by-step feature or onboarding walkthrough. The default mode. Its card shows progress, **Back**, **Next**, **Done** on the last step, and **Skip tour**.
- **Walkthrough** — an analysis, like board-meeting prep, with softer wording: **Continue**, **Done**, and **Exit walkthrough**.
- **Explain** — a single on-demand spotlight rather than a multi-step sequence, closed with one **Got it** button.

When Ari starts a tour or walkthrough, the chat shows a **Start tour** or **Start walkthrough** button so you choose when it begins; an explanation opens at once. A button in a Canvas block can also ask Ari for a saved Guide: it starts a new chat that sends the request at once, and the Guide then follows the same rule, an explanation opening right away and a tour or walkthrough waiting for its **Start tour** or **Start walkthrough** button in that chat.

## What a step can spotlight

Each step targets one of:

- A **named** UI element, like a sidebar toggle.
- A **block** on a Page.
- A single **cell**, a whole **row**, or a whole **column** on a Table Block, addressed by the Variables and Dimension items that identify it — including a Scenario comparison column.
- A **Canvas element** — one declared element inside a Canvas block, such as a KPI tile the Canvas source marks with a stable ID.
- **None** — a centered narration step with no on-screen target, useful for an introduction or a transition between sections.

A step whose target is not on screen is skipped, unless that target lives on another Page, in which case the Guide opens that Page first.

## Scripted steps

A step can also drive part of the app instead of only pointing at it. Ask Ari for a step that:

- **Shows the formula behind one cell**, for a step that explains how one number is calculated. Point it at that number's cell.
- **Shows where a table compares Scenarios**, opening the table's **Compare** popup without changing anything. It cannot run where the table offers no **Compare**: on a phone, for someone who cannot change the table, or in a published demo.
- **Lights a Canvas control for the viewer to try**, such as a slider, and can move on when the control reaches a value you name.
- **Opens version history on a recorded change**, to show that every change to the Model is recorded.
- **Walks through the Model**: what a Scenario is, then how Pages nest inside each other.
- **Opens the chat with prompts to try**, or **opens the Canvas library with templates picked**.

Ari writes these steps from your description; a step saved without its target is skipped until it has one. Most of them also run on their own from the **Help center** in your account menu (**⌘ ?**, or **Ctrl ?** on Windows and Linux): **Ask Ari to change your model**, **Start from a Canvas template**, and **Find your way around the model** under **Getting started**, **Compare scenarios side by side** and **See how a number is calculated** under **Tables & formulas**, and **Find and undo a change** under **Collaboration**. An entry that needs a Page or a table tells you to open one and start it again.

## Create a Guide

Ask Ari to save a new Guide with a title and its steps in order. For a Guide that targets blocks or cells, Ari uses the Scenario you're currently viewing so the targets and numbers line up when the Guide launches. Give each step a title and body, or richer content combining a heading, a label, and paragraphs. Line breaks typed in a step's body show in its card. A step can carry a short readable key, such as `roster`, so you can ask Ari to start the Guide at that step later.

## Run a Guide

Ask Ari to start a saved Guide by name, or to start it at a named step. Once running, ask Ari to move to the next or previous step, jump to a specific step, or dismiss it early.

When you start a Guide from the floating chat window over a full-screen Page, the chat moves out of the way of whatever a step spotlights and keeps clear of the coachmark, then returns to where you had it when the Guide ends. On a small screen where no position clears the spotlight, the chat hides for the rest of that Guide and comes back when the Guide ends; your conversation and any half-typed message are kept.

## Update or remove a Guide

Ask Ari to replace a Guide's title and steps, or delete one you no longer need.
