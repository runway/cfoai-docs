---
title: "Use Ari for formula work"
---

Ari can help you understand, test, and change formulas in CFO.ai. You can describe the business rule in plain language instead of writing the full expression yourself.

## What Ari can do

Ari can:

- Find and explain formulas on a Variable
- Check how a formula changes across segments or FormulaRanges
- Test a calculation without changing the model
- Validate formula syntax and references
- Create or update formulas
- Apply a formula to a table, segmentation, segment, Actuals, or Forecast
- Read calculated values after a change

## Decide whether to save

Tell Ari whether you are exploring or changing the model.

For analysis that should not be saved:

> Calculate contribution margin by Department without changing the model.

For a persistent change:

> Set Gross Profit to Revenue minus Cost of Goods Sold in the Base scenario.

If you do not want a write, say so directly. Ari can evaluate temporary formulas through the same calculation engine without creating a Variable, formula, or table.

## Give Ari useful context

Include these details when they matter:

- The target Variable
- The business calculation
- The table or page
- The segmentation or segment
- Actuals, Forecast, or all periods
- The scenario to use

You do not need to provide Variable IDs or formula conditions. Ari resolves those from the model.

## Review the result

When Ari reads or writes formulas, CFO.ai shows formula cards with the Variable, scope, and expression. Review those cards before moving on to a related change.
