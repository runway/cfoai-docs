Your workspace has a few settings that apply model-wide, rather than to any single Variable or Table Block. Ask Ari to change most of these directly.

## Rename the workspace

Ask Ari to rename your workspace. Renaming regenerates the workspace's URL slug, so any link built from the old slug changes along with it.

## Time defaults

Ask Ari to set the model's default time settings, or open **Settings → General** and use the **Date & Time** section yourself:

- **Default date granularity** — the default roll-up grain for new tables. Month unless you change it.
- **Default date range** — the default start and end date a new table shows.
- **Last close** — described below.

These are defaults for new Table Blocks; an existing table's own window (see [Build and format a Table Block](/dashboards/build-and-format-tables)) isn't affected until you change it directly.

## Last close

**Last close** is the date your Model computes Actuals against — everything at or before it is Actuals, and everything after is Forecast. Ask Ari to set it after each period closes in your books. Several other workflows, like [building a Headcount page](/headcount/build-a-headcount-page), depend on Last close being set correctly first.

## Team directory

Ask Ari to look up who is in the workspace, including current team members and pending invitations. Email addresses are shown only to people with workspace-wide access-control permission. People who opened a Page through an external link are counted but not identified.

Every member of a workspace is an administrator. There is no lower workspace role today, and there are no user groups. To give someone a report without making them an administrator, share the Page with an external link instead; see [Share a Page](/dashboards/share-pages-and-scenarios).

## Invite or remove workspace administrators

There is no team screen in Settings. Ask Ari, and when you have the required access it can:

- **Invite** someone as a workspace administrator. They receive an email invitation.
- **Promote** an existing member to administrator. This changes their role and sends no invitation.
- **Remove** a member or cancel a pending invitation. Ari refuses to remove you or the workspace's last administrator.

> Invite dana@example.com to the workspace.

> Who has a pending invitation, and can you cancel the one for the old contractor?

Ari cannot change a Page's access or create an external link on your behalf. Use the Page's **Share** control for that.
