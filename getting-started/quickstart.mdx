Ari is your AI finance coworker. Start with a decision, such as whether you can hire, when cash gets tight, or why expenses changed. Ari can build the Model behind the answer using connected data, an uploaded file, or clearly stated assumptions.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your workspace">
    Sign in at [app.cfo.ai](https://app.cfo.ai) with **Continue with Google**, **Sign in with Microsoft**, or your work email: enter it, select **Send code**, and type the six-digit code from the email. Your workspace holds your Model, Pages, Scenarios, data connections, and team settings.

    During onboarding, a new workspace opens in a chat named **Welcome**, with Ari's greeting and a starting card. cfo.ai starts researching you and your company as soon as you verify the email, with a public search for the email address and, when it is a work address, for the company behind its domain, and Ari posts a short company brief in the chat when that research finishes; while it is still running, the chat shows **Searching the web** in its place. The brief ends with a **Sources** list: your signup's email domain, then each public page the research used, linked by its site and labeled with what ties it to you, such as **shows your full email address**, **on your email domain**, **found by searching your email domain**, or **matches your name only**. For a personal email, such as a Gmail address, a page that matches only your name is left out, so the brief does not ask whether a stranger's profile is yours. Confirm or correct the brief. That context helps Ari choose a sensible starting point. The brief is researched for the person who created the workspace; a teammate who joins the workspace later sees the greeting and the starting card without a brief.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask a business question">
    Describe the decision that matters. You do not need to know which financial model or report to request.

    > Can we hire three engineers in September? Show me what happens to payroll, cash, and cash runway.

    Ari can use the current company context to determine which information and calculations the answer needs.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to begin">
    The starting card beside Ari's greeting asks two questions. It does not wait for the company brief, and answering either question and selecting **Get started** starts your first Model. You can also share a file or import an existing model.

    - **Where would you like to start?** Choose **My business** to plan the company Ari researched, **A different business** to describe another one, or **Just show me what you can do** for a small walkthrough model. **Other…** lets you type your own answer.
    - **Do you want to use your real numbers?** Choose **Connect my real data now** when accounting, HR, CRM, spreadsheet, or database history should inform the answer, or **Just estimate my numbers for now** to work from a business description and stated assumptions first.
    - **Import an existing model** when a workbook or report already contains the financial structure you want to rebuild: attach it to the chat instead of answering the card.

    Connecting data is optional. You can start with estimates and add the supporting systems later. The sidebar's **Model** section, with the Scenario picker and the list of Pages, appears once Ari creates your first Page.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the first result">
    Ari names the Pages your business needs, then builds them one at a time, so you can read the first Page while the next one forms; a forecast simulator and a walkthrough of the model follow the Pages. Review Ari's answer and check the sources, assumptions, date range, and business categories. If Ari creates a Page or Table Block, open it. Ask Ari to explain a number or adjust an assumption, or change the Model directly.

    > Show me the hiring assumptions, then move the start dates to October and compare the cash forecast.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide what comes next">
    Add another source, create a Scenario, prepare a report, or save a recurring workflow. You and Ari keep working from the same Model, so the answer remains visible and useful after the first conversation.
  </Step>
</Steps>
